## Coupling of the two halves

Forward-sensitive data (large abscissa α, e.g. chaotic Lorenz) and ill-conditioned libraries tend to coincide: the forward sensitivity of the dynamics lower-bounds how hard the inverse identification is. See `hadamard/plots/coupling.png`.
