Field equations

The swarm as smooth fields inside the square inscribed in the arena, smoothed over one cap diameter: density ρ (share of floor covered by caps), polarization P of the propulsion direction, and W, the polarization weighted by each robot's spin rate. These laws were found for this recording. The field simulation steps them every 0.08 s from the recorded start, adding the laws' recorded residual fields as noise. The agent simulations' trajectories are smoothed into fields the same way. In the spin-rate group model each spin direction is split into its slower- and faster-spinning half (four groups), each group has its own polarization field P_b, and the laws are found the same way for ρ and every P_b; P and W are then the sums over groups.

Spin-rate groups

Every robot's mean spin rate, sorted (positive = counter-clockwise). Each spin direction is split into its slower- and faster-spinning half. Solid line: the group's measured mean rate; dashed: the rotation rate in the group's field law.

Definitions

Robots and arena

φ
Marker heading from the video tracking (the tick on each robot).
ω
A robot's spin rate: the slope of φ over the whole recording, rad/s; positive = counter-clockwise.
θ, p
θ is the robot's drive direction relative to its marker; p = (cos(φ+θ), sin(φ+θ)) is the direction it propels itself.
cap
The cylindrical top, 48.7 mm across and 19.2 mm high; rigid caps cannot overlap. One camera scale, 45.08 px/cm, fits both arena diameters (40 and 41 cm).
σ
230 px ≈ 5.1 cm, the typical centre distance of two touching robots; contact counts use it.

Simulations

per-robot EPDE laws
Every robot runs the equations EPDE found for that robot alone.
common system
One law for the whole recording. Push, wall, persistence and the constants are shared; only each robot's own propulsion (speed and direction relative to its marker) is its own.
contact law
A push that grows as two robots close in, plus half the drive a neighbour pushes into the robot.
guard
Pairs are only kept from overlapping more deeply than the closest distance ever recorded (about 0.88–0.90 σ).
cap core
Caps cannot overlap: centres stay at least one cap diameter apart, as rigid cylinders do.
+ spin friction
Adds sliding friction between touching spinning caps and along the wall, with fitted coefficients. It has no measurable effect.
clock
Positions every 0.4 s, starting from the recorded positions at t = 9.6 s; the replay shows seed 0.

Swarm statistics

MSD
Mean squared displacement ⟨|r(t+τ) − r(t)|²⟩ over robots and start times. MSD error = mean |ln(MSD_sim / MSD_exp)| over lags 0.4–100 s.
NN distance
Distance from a robot's centre to its nearest neighbour's centre.
touching neighbours
Neighbours whose centres are closer than 1.1 σ (4 or more pooled).
radial layering
Distribution of r/R: r is the distance from the arena centre, R the largest distance a robot centre reaches.
speed by contact number
Mean of |Δr| / 0.4 s, grouped by the number of touching neighbours. Speed error = mean |ln(sim / exp)| over those groups.
TV
Total variation distance between two distributions, ½ Σ |p_k − q_k|: 0 when equal, 1 when disjoint.
corr MSD 10 s, radius, circulation
Correlation across robots, simulation against experiment, of each robot's 10 s MSD, its mean r/R, and its mean velocity around the arena centre.
held-out R²
A law fitted on the first half of the recording and scored on the second, on 0.4 s step velocities; the R² gain is the difference between two laws.

Fields

K, grid
Gaussian kernel with standard deviation one cap diameter (4.87 cm); fields on a 14 × 14 grid over the square inscribed in the arena, every 0.08 s.
ρ
Σ A K(r − r_i), A = cap area: the share of floor covered by caps.
P
Σ A p_i K(r − r_i): the local polarization of the propulsion directions.
W
Σ A ω_i p_i K(r − r_i): the polarization weighted by spin rate, rad/s.
spin-rate groups, P_b
Each spin direction split into its faster- and slower-spinning half (four groups); P_b is P over the robots of group b only, and P = Σ_b P_b.

Field laws

∂ₜf
Forward increment (f(t + 0.08 s) − f(t)) / 0.08 s.
∂ₓ, ∂ᵧ, ∇·, ∇²
Central differences on the grid; ∇·P = ∂ₓPₓ + ∂ᵧPᵧ, ∇² = ∂ₓ² + ∂ᵧ².
ẑ×P
P turned by +90°, (−Pᵧ, Pₓ); ∂ₜP = ω ẑ×P rotates P at rate ω.
balanced law
The member of the found front that is both precise and stable: its error (WAPE = Σ|y − ŷ| / Σ|y|) and its window instability (on a log scale) are each rescaled to 0–1 over the front, and the law closest to (0, 0) is chosen.
window instability
The law refit on windows a quarter of each axis long (time and both space directions); the summed squared relative spread (variance / mean²) of its coefficients.
v₀
Transport speed in ∂ₜρ = −v₀ ∇·P, cm/s.
D_r, ω_b, D_b
Decay rate of the polarization (1/s); rotation rate (rad/s) and decay rate (1/s) of group b's polarization, read from the eigenvalue of its 0.08 s step, which removes the step's own ω²·0.08/2.
κ, ρ̄(r), D
Density relaxation −κ(ρ − ρ̄(r)) toward each point's time-mean density ρ̄(r), and density diffusion D ∇²ρ (D in cm²/s).
noise
Each 0.08 s step of a field simulation adds the laws' recorded residual fields from one randomly drawn instant.

Field statistics

C_P(τ), C_W(τ)
⟨P(t)·P(t+τ)⟩ / ⟨|P|²⟩ over grid and time, sampled every 0.4 s; the same for W.
⟨Δρ²⟩(τ)
⟨(ρ(t+τ) − ρ(t))²⟩; it levels off when density fluctuations stay bounded.
δρ, |P|, |W|
Root mean square of the density's deviation from its time mean at each point, of |P| and of |W|.
C error
Mean |C(τ) − C_experiment(τ)| over lags 0.4–10 s.
