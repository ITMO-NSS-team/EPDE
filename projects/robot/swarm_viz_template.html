Field equations: hypothesis systems

The swarm as smooth fields over the whole arena, on rings and angles about its centre: density ρ (share of floor covered by caps), the polarization P of the propulsion direction split into its outward part P_r and its part around the centre P_θ, and W, the polarization weighted by each robot's spin rate, split the same way. Each hypothesis is a complete set of laws, one per field. Every law is a member of its equation's non-dominated front found by the search: the most stable front law with the hypothesis's property. A hypothesis no front law supports is marked so and not simulated. Each supported system is stepped every 0.4 s from the recorded start, with the laws' recorded residual fields, their time mean removed, as noise, so every steady structure comes from the laws. The agent simulations' trajectories are smoothed into fields the same way.

Density across the arena

Definitions

Robots and arena

φ
Marker heading from the video tracking (the tick on each robot).
ω
A robot's spin rate: the slope of φ over the whole recording, rad/s; positive = counter-clockwise.
θ, p
θ is the robot's drive direction relative to its marker; p = (cos(φ+θ), sin(φ+θ)) is the direction it propels itself.
cap
The cylindrical top, 48.7 mm across and 19.2 mm high; rigid caps cannot overlap. One camera scale, 45.08 px/cm, fits both arena diameters (40 and 41 cm).
σ
230 px ≈ 5.1 cm, the typical centre distance of two touching robots; contact counts use it.

Simulations

per-robot EPDE laws
Every robot runs the equations EPDE found for that robot alone.
common system
One law for the whole recording. Push, wall, persistence and the constants are shared; only each robot's own propulsion (speed and direction relative to its marker) is its own.
contact law
A push that grows as two robots close in, plus half the drive a neighbour pushes into the robot.
guard
Pairs are only kept from overlapping more deeply than the closest distance ever recorded (about 0.88–0.90 σ).
cap core
Caps cannot overlap: centres stay at least one cap diameter apart, as rigid cylinders do.
+ spin friction
Adds sliding friction between touching spinning caps and along the wall, with fitted coefficients. It has no measurable effect.
clock
Positions every 0.4 s, starting from the recorded positions at t = 9.6 s; the replay shows seed 0.

Swarm statistics

MSD
Mean squared displacement ⟨|r(t+τ) − r(t)|²⟩ over robots and start times. MSD error = mean |ln(MSD_sim / MSD_exp)| over lags 0.4–100 s.
NN distance
Distance from a robot's centre to its nearest neighbour's centre.
touching neighbours
Neighbours whose centres are closer than 1.1 σ (4 or more pooled).
radial layering
Distribution of r/R: r is the distance from the arena centre, R the largest distance a robot centre reaches.
speed by contact number
Mean of |Δr| / 0.4 s, grouped by the number of touching neighbours. Speed error = mean |ln(sim / exp)| over those groups.
TV
Total variation distance between two distributions, ½ Σ |p_k − q_k|: 0 when equal, 1 when disjoint.
corr MSD 10 s, radius, circulation
Correlation across robots, simulation against experiment, of each robot's 10 s MSD, its mean r/R, and its mean velocity around the arena centre.
held-out R²
A law fitted on the first half of the recording and scored on the second, on 0.4 s step velocities; the R² gain is the difference between two laws.

Fields

K, grid
Gaussian kernel with standard deviation one cap diameter (4.87 cm); fields on 10 rings, from 1 cm out to R (the largest distance a robot centre reaches from the arena centre), × 24 angles, every 0.08 s and used every 0.4 s.
ρ
Σ A K(r − r_i), A = cap area: the share of floor covered by caps.
P, P_r, P_θ
P = Σ A p_i K(r − r_i), the local polarization of the propulsion directions; P_r is its outward component at each grid point, P_θ its component around the centre (the direction of increasing angle in the image).
W, W_r, W_θ
Σ A ω_i p_i K(r − r_i): the polarization weighted by spin rate (rad/s), split the same way. A mean W_θ above zero near the wall is a spin-weighted circulation along the wall.

Field laws

∂ₜf
Forward increment (f(t + Δt) − f(t)) / Δt over the fit step Δt = 0.4 s. A law fitted at Δt is stepped at Δt.
fit step
A one-step fit sees only what changes within Δt. At 0.08 s it misses decays that build up over about a second (polarization drifting out of phase, the density's restoring).
∂_r, (1/r)∂_θ, ∇·P, ∇²
Radial and angular differences on the polar grid (periodic in angle, one-sided at the first and the last ring); ∇·P = ∂_rP_r + P_r/r + (1/r)∂_θP_θ, ∇² = ∂_r² + (1/r)∂_r + (1/r²)∂_θ².
rotation coupling
∂ₜP_r = … − c W_θ and ∂ₜP_θ = … + c W_r turn P with the spin (ẑ×W in polar components); the same pair links W back to P.
front
The search's laws for one field that no other law beats on both error (WAPE = Σ|y − ŷ| / Σ|y|) and window instability, listed from most stable to most precise.
window instability
The law refit on windows a quarter of each axis long (time, rings and angles); the summed squared relative spread (variance / mean²) of its coefficients.
hypothesis system
One law per field, each the most stable front law with the hypothesis's property. H1: density carried by ∇·P without relaxation; P and W decay and rotate into each other. H2: H1 with −κ(ρ − ρ̄). H3: H2 with a W_θ law driven by the density's shape (∂ρ, ∇²ρ) or the distance from the centre (r, 1/r, a constant).
κ, ρ̄(r)
Density relaxation −κ(ρ − ρ̄(r)) toward each point's time-mean density ρ̄(r); the search finds it with equal and opposite coefficients on ρ and ρ̄.
noise
Each 0.4 s step adds the laws' recorded residual fields from one randomly drawn instant, with each field's time mean removed (that mean is the laws' systematic misfit).
diverges
The law, stepped every 0.4 s, makes a field grow without bound (|field| above 100).

Field statistics

C_P(τ), C_W(τ)
⟨P(t)·P(t+τ)⟩ / ⟨|P|²⟩, area-weighted over the arena, sampled every 0.4 s; the same for W.
⟨Δρ²⟩(τ)
⟨(ρ(t+τ) − ρ(t))²⟩, area-weighted; it levels off when density fluctuations stay bounded.
δρ, |P|, |W|
Root mean square of the density's deviation from its time mean at each point, of |P| and of |W|.
ρ(r), W_θ(r)
Radial profiles: means over time and angle on each ring.
C error
Mean |C(τ) − C_experiment(τ)| over lags 0.4–10 s.
