Joint prediction-risk fitting: tested, mixed gains, no structural recovery gain
2026-11-16

Completed the default 15 systems at 5% noise, seeds 0/1/2: 45 system runs,
20 equation slots and 60 equation/seed cases. The primary joint-risk selector
retains registered structures in 54/60 cases, exactly the same cases as the
existing local continuous CLSM fit with local held-out discrepancy and dyad.
This is a finite-candidate Pareto-panel result, not 54 accurately recovered
equations or a new evolutionary-search benchmark. Registered equivalents
are credited, and slots of multi-equation systems are assessed separately.

The new method selects between local/broad-minimum-q/broad-q+1 weak fitting
banks and OLS/CLSM estimators. It jointly perturbs target and predictor moments,
refits coefficients, and estimates noise-corrected prediction risk plus
conditional bootstrap optimism on a COMMON LOCAL training weak bank. There
are 128 shared operator-error draws. No clean or held-out score selects a fit.
The original 32/128-draw prefix still defines the point-fit correction. All
options and ablations were frozen before testing; see protocol.txt.

The four difficult systems were tested first. Their KS preservation and
partial forced-KdV gains justified running the unchanged method on the other
11 default systems. This sequential expansion is exploratory and reuses the
same noise seeds; it is not an independent confirmation dataset.

Primary structural result, local cross discrepancy plus fixed dyad:
  Existing local CLSM                         54/60
  Joint prediction risk, all fitting options  54/60
  Joint prediction risk, CLSM options only    54/60
  Joint prediction risk, OLS options only     53/60
  Prediction-spread-only selection            53/60
The primary 64-draw average also retains 54/60, with the same failure cases.
The separate ordinary-score control increases 53 -> 54/60. The old spectral
cross-score control increases 53 -> 54/60, but is not the primary comparison.

Unresolved cases are AC seeds 0 and 2, forced KdV seeds 0/1/2, and KS seed 0.
All are slot 0. There are no new primary structural losses or recoveries.

Accuracy uses residual energy of the fitted reference coefficients on the
same frozen clean bank, evaluated only AFTER selection. Lower is better.
Across 60 cases it improves in 26, worsens in 16, and is exactly unchanged in
18. These counts include small changes and are not significance claims.

Forced-KdV median energy improves by about 38%, and compound PDE and van der
Pol improve in all three seeds. However, forced KdV remains inaccurate and
its registered structure still fails all three cases. Improvements in median
accuracy can also hide regressions: Duffing seed 0 worsens 5.30x, from
1.53894e-5 to 8.15698e-5; division-PDE seed 0 worsens 3.74x, from 7.64061e-6
to 2.85494e-5. Both still retain their registered structures. These ratios
start from small errors; they are not catastrophic failures on the scale of
the earlier broad-bank KS results. See accuracy_comparison.csv and
accuracy_per_slot.csv for every case/slot, rather than just these examples.

The important positive result is KS: all nine reference fits choose local
CLSM and preserve its previous accuracy. Unlike predictor-only information
selection, common-bank prediction risk recognizes the large residual error
of broad fits. For seed 0/fold 0, local CLSM has estimated risk -0.0001907
and local clean energy 7.56e-6; broad-minimum-q CLSM has risk 0.27663 and local
clean energy 0.27631. Signed negative risk estimates are retained as specified.
This supports including the complete fitted residual, but does not by itself
prove the bootstrap optimism component is what prevents the KS failure.

Wave supplies a direct counterexample to the risk ranking. Seed 0/fold 0:
  Option             coefficient  estimated risk  local clean energy
  Local CLSM         0.0481738     0.0098484       0.0416918
  Broad-min-q OLS    0.0408289     0.0376281       0.00042494
The true coefficient is 0.04. The selector prefers the
less accurate fit; the same ordering error exists on the fixed spectral
clean bank. It therefore cannot be explained by comparing two different
evaluation representations. The local training proxy and its noise/bias
correction misrank these options. This is consistent with the previously
diagnosed reconstruction target error; these data do not uniquely isolate
every source of risk-estimation bias. Conditional variance modeling cannot
guarantee correction of a common reconstruction bias. No calibrated
confidence interpretation is assigned to these risk estimates.

Some forced-KdV perturbation fits are also sensitive. Seed 0/fold 0 broad-min-q
CLSM has estimated risk 0.7224 but optimism Monte Carlo SE 1.2462. The SE is
conditional and is not an uncertainty interval for physical-model validity
or for a paired difference between fitting options. Half/full comparisons
change 198/3150 candidate/fold choices, including 8/180 reference choices,
while leaving all primary retention indicators unchanged. Half/full averaging
shares fitted coefficients, noise covariance, data, and nested draws; it
checks numerical averaging sensitivity, not independent replication.

Validation and provenance:
All 45 cases reproduce the saved local baseline scores exactly (maximum
scaled difference 0). Stored point-coefficient checks pass. Source and input
hash checks pass. All score and risk entries are finite, and all 270 proposed
broad fold/bank arms are feasible under the frozen numerical-rank rule.
Fixed-X optimism equals 2*sigma_eff^2*rank(X), fixed-coefficient mean optimism
is zero to rounding, and feature-unit rescaling/solver agreement checks pass.
These are mathematical and implementation checks, not evidence that the
conditional model captures all real reconstruction errors.

An initial equivalent arithmetic implementation failed the predefined
coefficient-reproduction check on an ill-conditioned extra AC candidate.
The original operation order was restored, without changing the statistical
method or tolerance. The seven earlier results and original source/checks
are preserved separately and excluded from the 45 reported runs. Details
are in implementation_notes.txt. Post-selection option diagnostics cover
the 12 targeted cases and are independently saved with their input hashes.

Recommendation: keep local continuous CLSM plus local held-out discrepancy
and dyad as the working baseline. Preserve joint risk as an experimental
fitting selector with partial accuracy gains; do not promote it as a fix for
the remaining failures. The next focused test should examine validation
moment bias, especially wave: compare reconstructed moments against independent
raw-observation weak moments for fixed candidate coefficients. Prior raw-data
coefficient fitting already failed as a universal replacement; validating
existing fits with raw moments is a different, as-yet untested use. Its
quadrature error and variance must be measured explicitly. Do not substitute
another arbitrary stability threshold for this bias diagnosis.

All work used saved fields, reconstruction, weak quadrature and linear
algebra. No candidate equation integration, PINNs, clean-data regeneration,
production edits, or commits. Worker processes have finished. Code, per-case
results, audit JSON, CSV summaries and scientific figures are in this folder.
Remaining limits include frozen bootstrap correction maps, reused records,
three seeds, a finite candidate pool and conditional rather than calibrated
uncertainty.
