Numerical reproduction correction

The first implementation used algebraically equivalent einsum/centered-product
arithmetic for the covariance correction and a precomputed feature norm.
AC seed 1 fold 1 contained an ill-conditioned extra candidate whose coefficient
differed from the stored baseline by 8.07e-8 (relative 1.09e-6), exceeding the
predefined reproduction tolerance. The check stopped that worker.

The correction preserves the original sequential accumulation, matrix-product
ordering, and point-fit helper. The statistical criterion, all options,
selection rule, noise draws, and tolerances were unchanged. Earlier source,
checks, and seven completed results are retained as run_joint_initial.py,
initial_checks.json, and initial_results/. They are excluded from the reported
experiment. The complete targeted suite is rerun under one corrected source.

The fast normalized solver remains in use for new conditional perturbation
fits, where no previous baseline values exist. Its agreement with the existing
helper and feature-rescaling invariance are checked before every worker runs.
