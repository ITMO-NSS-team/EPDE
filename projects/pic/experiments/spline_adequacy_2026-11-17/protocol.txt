Automatic mean adequacy and noise-information screening
=====================================================
Preserve all previous results and production files. No ODE/PDE solver, removed
constants/identities, clipped noise parameters, or discarded observations.
Development used only prior stress seeds1283,1334,1456,1016; both older boundary
failures were repaired, but this is not validation. Original fixed candidate
splines p8,16,24,32,48,64, rho grid and CV score remain unchanged.

For each p compare its mean space with span(Xp,Xq), where q is the next grid
dimension; q96 is the diagnostic extension at p64. These uniform cubic spaces
share the four global cubics, so union rank is p+q-4 (verified numerically).
At a fixed estimated rho compute generalized-least-squares residual sums of
squares Q0,Q1 and F=((Q0-Q1)/(r-p))/(Q1/(n-r)). The main adjacent-noise version
uses rho from the q fit (p64 supplies rho for its q96 extension). The own-noise
version uses rho from p. Rho is estimated, so the nominal F p-value is an
adequacy diagnostic, not an exact calibrated test. Retain source-fit warnings.

For each candidate use its own restricted likelihood and analytic positive-
boundary limit rho->1, sigma^2(1-rho)->eta. Require limiting LR > chi-square2
(.975)+1e-6. This excludes the known escaping sequence, but does not by itself
certify compactness of the whole noise region. Do not shrink the parameter
domain. Require the selected fit and screening-noise fit to have status ok.
Eligible candidates also need adequacy p-value >= alpha. Select either best
original CV score (ties smaller p) or smallest eligible p. If none qualifies,
abstain with unresolved_no_candidate; keep the observation in the denominator.
Compare alpha .01 and .05, own and adjacent rho, CV and smallest orderings:
eight frozen alternatives. Primary candidate: adjacent rho, CV order, alpha.01.
Unchanged CV and fixed-p32 are comparators. No method is chosen after validation.

Fresh benchmark cohort264: ODE seeds1800..1863, VDP1800..1815, Duffing1800..1807,
all iid,+.4,-.4. Existing raw RNG domain984101 and5% signal-standard-deviation

f=1,2,4,6,8,12; each profile iid,+.4,-.4,16 realizations. Noise5% signal std;

Exact-model controls256: zero mean, sigma1, n320/1001, rho=-.4,0,.4,.8,32 fields
each; RNG[991102,n,rho_index,replicate]. Total1832 independent noisy fields.
Fit/selection records for ALL fields frozen before truth-based scoring.

Noise-region diagnostic: for each chosen p, restricted likelihood-ratio region
with nominal chi-square2(.975) cutoff; evaluate membership at true sigma,rho.
Model selection is not accounted for by that cutoff. Report empirical coverage,
abstentions, boundary warnings and analytic unboundedness separately, with full
denominators. Do not claim calibrated97.5% confidence or boundedness merely
because the screening rule was satisfied. No new coefficient propagation yet.

likelihoods and boundary covariance limits. Audit every field/hash, all fitted
