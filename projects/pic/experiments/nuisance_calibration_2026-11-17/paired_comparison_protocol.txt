Paired comparison on the24 bootstrap validation fields
====================================================
Before their bootstrap regions are scored, also register evaluation of the
already-frozen nominal/model-calibrated LR and nominal Wald procedures on the
same24 observed fields. Reuse their saved original guarded fits. Do not refit
or tune the calibration cutoffs; they remain the controlled-model cutoffs.
This permits a paired method comparison instead of comparing192-field and
24-field cohorts as if their sampling variability were the same. Evaluate only
after all24 bootstrap regions are frozen. Preserve this as a separate result.
