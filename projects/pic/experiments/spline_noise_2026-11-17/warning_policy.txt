Implementation clarification after inspecting bootstrap warnings
==============================================================
The frozen protocol already says selected-fit warnings invalidate nominal
numerical status and that draws must never be removed. Two spline refits in
positive/402 (draws46 and76) selected p16 and hit rho=0.999999, the declared
search endpoint. These are retained as boundary warnings, not clipped away,
replaced, or re-estimated under a changed selection rule.

Continue every predeclared calculation using ALL128 refits, including the
continuous basic ellipse and all66 finite scenarios when mathematically
defined. Those algebraic quantities and geometry remain useful diagnostics.
For a case with any selected-fit warning, report nominal noise/weak-error
coverage as unresolved (null), preserve the computed membership separately
as diagnostic_coverage, and exclude its width from valid paired-width
comparisons while counting it in the full12-case denominator. Do not claim
that its computed bounded enclosure is a validated uncertainty result.
No fitting, selection, cutoff, draw or noise-scenario rule is changed.

This clarification is recorded before any downstream weak-error scoring.
It implements the original warning rule; it is not a repaired estimator.
