Exact covariance calculation used in this experiment
---------------------------------------------------
For an n-sample stationary AR(1) correlation matrix R(rho), orthonormal
DCT-II coordinates turn R^-1 into diagonal D plus a rank-two endpoint term:
  D[k] = (1 + rho^2 - 2*rho*cos(pi*k/n)) / (1-rho^2),
  Q = D + [rho/(1+rho)] * U*U^T,
where U contains the DCT transforms of the first and last unit vectors.
The stable implementation uses squared sine/cosine forms of the numerator.
This is an exact finite-domain identity, checked against dense matrices.

For a separable tensor covariance, full precision is the Kronecker product
of the per-axis precisions. Let L be the tensor of low DCT frequencies and
O its complement. The marginal precision on O is
  P_O = Q_OO - Q_OL * Q_LL^-1 * Q_LO.
Since L is a tensor block, Q_LL is also a Kronecker product. Set discarded
coefficients tozero in z. The retained quadratic is
  z^T Q z - (Qz)_L^T Q_LL^-1 (Qz)_L.
The retained covariance determinant follows
  logdet(C_O) = logdet(C_full) + logdet(Q_LL).
All per-axis actions/inverses use a diagonal plus rank-two matrix, avoiding
dense matrices at the largest KS field sizes. No diagonal-frequency noise
approximation is made.

For the corner control H (high on every axis), each marginal high block has
precision D_H+U_H*K*U_H^T with
  K = alpha*(I + alpha*U_L^T*D_L^-1*U_L)^-1,
  alpha = rho/(1+rho).
The tensor corner covariance remains separable. This makes the same exact
quadratic/logdet calculation inexpensive, but discards informative strips.

With nO retained coefficients, sigma^2 is profiled as q/nO and the objective
is log(q/nO)+logdet(C_O)/nO. The three-start optimizer estimates all axis rhos
jointly. The method assumes that retained signal coefficients are negligible.
Failure of that assumption is a modeling error even when covariance algebra
and optimization are exact. The compound-system attribution demonstrates
one consequence: a wrong covariance contraction changes the mean weak matrix.

The approximate rho standard deviations saved with fits are local curvature
diagnostics of this Gaussian profile likelihood. They are not calibrated
confidence intervals and are not used to select a noise model or test cutoff.

Follow-up priorities after this frozen comparison
------------------------------------------------
Keep the known-iid estimator when that family is specified by the benchmark.
Do not automatically switch noise models by achieved reference coverage.
For unknown correlation, improve nuisance signal treatment while retaining
frequencies that identify correlation. A joint signal/covariance restricted
likelihood is a more targeted next hypothesis than another instability score.
Boundary trends and signal leakage into frequency strips need explicit tests.
The corner-only control is not a general fix: it loses correlation information.

Then propagate noise-parameter uncertainty, currently held fixed during
weak-moment calibration. Test on fresh raw fields and on pure-noise controls,
with both correlation signs and short ODE records. Any covariance union or
likelihood confidence region should remain an uncertainty calculation, not
a structural ranking reward for larger or less identifiable equations.
