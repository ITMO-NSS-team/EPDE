Completed 2026-11-17: joint spectral noise estimation for solver-free moments.

The new joint correlation/amplitude estimator substantially repairs positive-
correlation coverage on the tested PDEs, but is NOT a universal replacement.
On fresh anchors, positive-rho operator coverage improves from36.07/59.08%
with the existing AR estimator to97.10/97.56% (local/broad). On the full iid
suite, broad coverage instead falls94.64 ->87.80%. We traced an important
failure to wrong nonlinear moment corrections caused by signal leakage into
the noise estimate. Short one-dimensional contrasts also lose too much
correlation information. Keep the known-iid path for the current iid benchmark.

Design, scope and independence
-----------------------------
Primary noise estimator fits stationary separable AR(1) correlation and a
common amplitude within each variable jointly, using exact Gaussian profile
likelihood for high-frequency DCT-II contrasts. Remove the tensor block of
the lower half of frequencies on every axis, retaining the complementary
frequency strips. Each variable has its own amplitude and per-axis rhos.
There is no minimum across axis amplitudes or differencing orders, no signal
variance cap, and no supplied true amplitude/correlation.

The covariance in DCT coordinates is evaluated exactly using diagonal plus
rank-two precision matrices and a Schur complement. It is not a diagonal
periodogram approximation. Details/derivation are in method_notes.txt.
This is solver-free with respect to candidate dynamics: only transforms,
linear algebra and low-dimensional statistical optimization are used.

Prespecified corner-only control retains frequencies high on every axis.
It passes covariance algebra checks but fails noise identification in a
synthetic positive-rho example: sigma2.884 instead of0.2. Its failure is
preserved in checks.json; the initial all-design adequacy assertion is
retained in checks_initial.py. The original primary strips design remains
primary throughout, with no per-system choice or cutoff adjustment.

Compound failure attribution: covariance error changes the mean
---------------------------------------------------------------
For iid compound anchors33/34/35, the spectral estimator infers spurious
time correlation about0.245/0.250/0.249 and spatial correlation0.045..0.056.
Its sigmas are0.010398/0.010511/0.010482. Fitting the exact injected noise
instead gives0.008752/0.008910/0.008795 with rhos close tozero. This paired
raw/noise-only comparison supports signal leakage into the spectral estimate.

For polynomial columns of degree<=3, mean corrected-matrix error under
covariance misspecification can be evaluated from clean fields as
  corrected_M(clean,assumed_cov) - corrected_M(clean,true_cov).
Its whitened broad operator score is4.29/4.15/4.29 TIMES the saved test cutoff.
The empirical actual-field mean errors agree with this predicted bias, with
finite Monte Carlo residuals explicitly saved. Calibration assumes the fitted
covariance; it therefore does not anticipate this actual-field mean shift.

Keep the spectral pilot/calibration/whitening/cutoff and all256 actual fields
unchanged; use the injected covariance ONLY for correcting the actual fields.
Broad coverage changes0/256 ->256/256 on EACH of the three anchors. All1536
local/broad oracle operator scores are independently replayed. This is a
cause-isolating oracle control, not a deployable fix or an independent sample.
It also leaves model-variance misspecification in place, so100% coverage is
not evidence that every part of the spectral model is now correct.

The failure is more specific than simply underestimating noise amplitude:
a wrong correlation model can bias nonlinear Gaussian contractions even when
the inferred marginal amplitude is larger than the true one. Inflating a
scalar residual threshold or preferring a more stable coefficient ratio
would not address that modeling error directly.

Short-series information-loss diagnostic
-----------------------------------------
576 fresh pure-noise draws: n=301/320/1001 x rho0,+0.4,-0.4 x64 draws.
Compare spectral strips with exact full-sample Gaussian AR fitting when
zero mean/no signal is known. The latter is an attribution oracle and is
not proposed for raw EPDE data. Neither fit receives the true rho or sigma.

Spectral reaches the stationarity boundary in26/576 draws:3/192 iid,
23/192 positive-rho,0/192 negative-rho. Positive-rho breakdown by length:
15/64 at301,5/64 at320,3/64 at1001. The boundary implies an unstable amplitude
extrapolation; the numerical rho limit is not a meaningful finite uncertainty
bound. Boundary fits are reported, never silently clipped into successful
model selection or replaced with the iid model.

For positive rho, correlation RMSE is0.413/0.357/0.327 for spectral versus
0.052/0.057/0.025 for the full-noise oracle, at the three lengths. On iid
noise the respective values are0.306/0.261/0.126 versus0.050/0.057/0.030.
The loss occurs without signal, supporting insufficient correlation
information in the retained high frequencies, rather than only leakage.

Fresh raw-fit local curvature diagnostics are consistent with this:
median approximate rho SD0.143 in one-dimensional variables versus0.0083
across multidimensional axes. These are uncalibrated likelihood-curvature
diagnostics, not confidence intervals or model-selection gates. The current
fixed-parameter weak bootstrap does not propagate their uncertainty.

Decision
--------
Keep the known-iid amplitude estimator for the specified iid benchmark.
Keep the joint spectral estimator as a research candidate for unknown
correlation on multidimensional fields; these core4 results support that
direction but do not establish a general production default.

The next targeted repair is joint nuisance-signal/covariance estimation that
retains informative frequencies, followed by propagation of noise-parameter
uncertainty. It must address both leakage-induced correction bias and weak
correlation identification in short ODE records. No per-system oracle model
choice, arbitrary ridge/penalty, new instability objective or candidate
dynamics solver was introduced. Production and prior completed studies remain
unchanged; dyad remains the provisional structural objective.
