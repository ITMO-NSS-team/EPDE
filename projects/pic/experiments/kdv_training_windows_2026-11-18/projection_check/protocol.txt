Independent follow-up on the same two fixed trajectories after the initial 24-pair screen

Freeze 24 further forced seeds 790001–790024 and unforced seeds 791001–791024. Use the same 5% iid noise, rotated measurements, noise correction, full 12 candidates, and common forced held-outs. Retain the original unforced local and time windows in the main comparisons. Evaluate the original forced training banks, the wide spatial bank alone, and the same bank plus its projection onto the measured forcing. Give the wide-only bank twice its per-bank weight to preserve the previous total of two banks per record. The wide-only window geometry is identical across all candidates and uses neither the forcing column nor its profile to define its weights. The forcing term remains available as a candidate in every arm. The projection is an auxiliary training measurement.

Cross the three forced designs with joint or staged fitting. In staged fitting learn the intrinsic coefficient direction solely from the unforced trajectory and estimate the relative input and extra coefficients on the forced trajectory. As a replication, also run the earlier staged design with both trajectory window changes. Compare per-field addition wins, retention after each genuine deletion, median reference coefficient estimates, their absolute errors, and held-out discrepancies. Use the whole field as the paired unit. For the direct comparison between wide and projected windows report wins/ties/losses on field-level numbers of additions beating the law and the median paired reference-score difference.
