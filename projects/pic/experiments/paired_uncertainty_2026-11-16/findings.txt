Paired discrepancy uncertainty: all 15 systems, 3 seeds, 5% noise
Completed 2026-11-16. Solver-free; coefficients, dyad and test banks fixed.

The remaining failed comparisons are small relative to modeled evaluation
noise. This supports testing a lower-variance discrepancy representation;
it does not establish a new recovery result or validate a confidence rule.

All 45 system runs completed, covering 60 equation-seed cases. Replayed score
values reproduce the previous full continuous-bank calculation to a maximum
scaled error of 9.48e-10. Retention is unchanged at 53/60. No interval or
standardized margin was used to change the Pareto front.

Across the seven previously failed equation-seed cases there are 16
unregistered candidates dominating the primary reference. Every one has
a conditional 95% paired model interval crossing zero. Their absolute
standardized advantages range from 0.024 to 1.407. Seven of these 16
competitors actually have worse fixed-fit clean residual scores.

There are five additional primary-reference dominance comparisons involving
registered equivalent laws. Those are not discovery failures. In total all
21 primary-reference dominators have intervals crossing zero; do not count
the five registered alternatives as false laws.

Pairing is essential. For KS seed 0, treating candidate scores independently
would give SD 0.2163 instead of 1.489e-5: the large common residual noise
cancels in the candidate difference. For AC seed 0, independent SD is 0.001017
versus paired 0.00009774, a factor of 10.4. Independent rotation errors also
omit covariance because each raw group appears in two held-out products.

This is not merely a model that makes all comparisons ambiguous. Of 116
primary comparisons whose competitor has no larger dyad, 67 have model
95% intervals excluding zero. All 67 favor the reference, and all have the
same sign on the fixed-fit clean control. The other 49 remain unresolved by
that descriptive interval. These are correlated, reused comparisons, not
116 independent trials or a validated multiple-testing procedure.

Calibration remains incomplete. Only 865/990 primary comparison intervals
contain their clean-oracle gap (87.4%, below the nominal 95% label). Exact-law
control intervals contain the clean score in 59/60 equation-seed cases.
Wave seed 2 misses: noisy exact-law score 0.02126; interval [0.001064,0.03920];
clean score 2.45e-8. These outcomes warn against treating the local model
intervals as calibrated discovery confidence.

Forced KdV exact-law scores +1996.4, +1458.8, -1337.7 have conditional model
SDs about 2428.8, 3803.9, 2084.3. Their intervals contain the near-zero clean
score, but are far too broad to deliver useful precision. Adding error bars
has diagnosed the problem; it has not repaired the discrepancy estimator.

Covariance-draw sensitivity on the 16 failed-case dominators: using 64
instead of 128 bootstrap covariance draws changes SD ratios to 0.943-1.105.
All 16 intervals still cross zero. The finite Monte Carlo and noise-model
assumptions remain; this check only addresses one numerical sensitivity.

Method and limitations:
- 128 reconstructed bootstrap operator draws per raw group; 512 joint
  symmetrized empirical perturbations, sharing group indices/signs across
  candidates and rotations. All cross-operator/moment correlations retained.
- Cross-products include linear and noise-times-noise contributions.
- Coefficients, training denominator, chosen supports and bands are fixed.
  Refitting/selection variability and common reconstruction bias are omitted.
- Group independence and the bootstrap-mean variance factor are model
  assumptions. The intervals are local conditional sensitivity diagnostics.
- Clean fields and exact coefficients are diagnostics only; no tuning uses
  them. No ODE/PDE candidate is integrated or new data generated.

Validation: exact scalar and complex-vector enumeration confirms paired
variance, quadratic contributions and shared-rotation covariance. Identical
candidates have zero paired perturbation. Complex covariance max error is
3.56e-15 across 216 exhaustive joint draws. All result/input/source hashes,
finite samples, frozen scores and unchanged fronts pass the audit.

One deterministic NS seed 1 case was redundantly executed by overlapping
workers; only one identical-seed result is counted. This supplies no extra
statistical replication. Calculation-seconds in summary.json sums unique
saved cases, not every redundant execution.
