Diagnostic of coefficient and held-out variability

On each system's 128 prospective fields, keep each set of three fitted full/reduced coefficient pairs together. Keep each set of three held-out cross-products, normalization scales and whitening together. Score coefficients from even-indexed realizations on held-out data from odd-indexed realizations and then reverse the split. Both tables cross 64 independent coefficient donors with 64 independent held-out donors from the same underlying trajectory. No donor contributes to both sides of a table. The physical geometry and fold ordering are common across donors. This uses stored estimates and no new fits.

For each table report its fraction of positive margins and the proportions of its empirical variance given by the coefficient-row means, held-out-column means, and remaining interaction. These three components add to one for the Cartesian product of the two donor lists. The diagonal from the original matched pipeline is separately replayed. Crossed combinations are dependent and are not additional independent benchmark trials. The decomposition describes the crossed table, not the sampling variance of the matched pipeline. Use the same calculation for the earlier Allen–Cahn benchmark and the new Navier–Stokes benchmark. This diagnostic does not modify the primary rule.
