Solver-free KdV comparison with a known forcing input

Test u_t = a*u*u_x + b*u_xxx + c*f on two stored trajectories. The registered kdV (PDE-FIND) record has f identically zero. The registered kdv_cossin record has f = cos(t)sin(x). Both benchmark definitions use a=-6 and b=-1. Coefficients are shared across records. Different spatial domains, initial/boundary data and the imposed forcing can all change which terms are measurable. This comparison cannot isolate a causal effect of changing the forcing alone.

Use all three matching iid seed pairs (51, 52, 53) in full_pipeline_uncertainty, containing six archived observations at 5% noise. Compare each forced observation, its unforced counterpart and the pair. Preserve each record's native local and broad measurements and use each record's 126th of 128 bootstrap maxima across its two banks (ceil(0.975*129)). This splits a nominal 5% error budget between two trajectories. Bootstrap calibration remains empirical. Use this same cutoff in single-record controls for attribution. Pair matching is by seed with separate system noise streams. The three pairs share the same two clean trajectories.

For the pair and the forced record test the full support, each single deletion, and a free dispersion-only model. Also test two fixed vectors, the registered physical law and the dispersion-only coefficient -1/5 discovered on the forced record. The unforced fit omits the identically zero forcing column. Accept unrestricted fits only with a finite explicitly checked witness, exclude only using a nonnegative positive-definite combination of the original homogeneous quadratic forms, and retain unresolved results. Check the certificates using exact rational arithmetic on the stored binary64 measurements, covariances and cutoffs. Term count is not used. All inputs are archived measurements; this is a posthoc cross-input test without candidate dynamics integration.
