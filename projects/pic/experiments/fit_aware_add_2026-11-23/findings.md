# Historical fit-aware operators and their transfer to nonlinear diffusion

The October campaign had already tested additions guided by the reduction in weighted squared residual (PVRA), additions screened by residual and then scored on both objectives (PVXA), instability-guided removal, pair additions, and fill-and-prune. Plain-mode NSGA-II in the current diffusion experiment already uses an all-library residual beam with probabilistic pruning. A new single-term mutation needs to demonstrate a gain over that baseline.

## Historical endpoints

The October campaign used 150 epochs. Exact Lotka–Volterra systems appeared on the final front in 2/3 PV baseline runs, 3/3 PVRA runs and 3/3 PVXA runs. The median first exact epoch among successes was 32, 17 and 12 respectively. For Lorenz the final-front counts including accepted identities were 2/3, 3/3 and 3/3. None of these three arms kept the canonical Lorenz system on the final front. PVRA generated it in one run. The subsequent universal search combined several changes. Its broader recovery cannot be assigned to the local addition alone. ## Matched live comparison

Six fresh noisy nonlinear-diffusion fields provided early and late discovery halves. Each of the twelve pairs used an identical field, native weak-window geometry, WAPE and directional dyad. Both arms retained native fill-and-prune at its configured probability of 0.25. In the experimental arm the ordinary addition used the archived PVRA chooser, sampling proportionally to gain among the best eight terms, with 10% ordinary exploration. It considered the permitted constant states. The search saw only the training half. Native weighted least-squares coefficients stayed frozen on the opposite half. No known structure was inserted in either archive. The two directions reuse the six fields.

The four successes occurred on the same fields. PVRA reached the law sooner on every one. In seed order, the first scoring calls were 231→83, 886→492, 354→292 and 91→50. Their median was 292.5 with native search and 187.5 with the guided addition. These figures condition on discovery. Across all ten-epoch runs the guide installed 9,006 terms and screened 2,856,446 candidate columns across intercept states. The median paired elapsed-time ratio was 1.23 in concurrently run jobs. The 6000-call comparison equalizes full scoring calls. The additional screening incurs real cost.

## Why additions missed

After discovery, a separate replay rebuilt each training field's native weak geometry and the historical candidate library. It supplied the physical time target and one correct predictor at a time, then measured the rank of its missing partner among 169 admissible terms. This counterfactual tests the addition rule in a favorable parent. It does not measure how frequently mutation visited that parent.

On the four successful early halves the missing predictor ranked first from either parent. On the two unsuccessful early halves the ranks were ninth and tenth respectively. On five of six late halves both possible completions fell outside the top eight, ranking from 10th to 43rd. On the sixth, both ranked seventh, each with about a 0.11 chance per ordinary addition through the guided path given the corresponding parent. That search also failed to generate the law. Unguided draws, swaps and beam fills can still reach proposals outside the shortlist.

The observed benefit is earlier generation when the weak data already rank the complementary physical term highly. A comparison with the historical pair-add operator (PVPA) would test whether joint gain can surface terms that the single-term screen misses. PVXA uses the same top-eight first stage as PVRA. It changes how candidates inside that shortlist compete. These experiments do not establish a universal mutation setting.

## Reproduction

Run `operator_trial.py`, `evaluate_trial.py` and `probe_add.py` with the repository environment. The runner loads the historical `arm_pvguide.py` chooser (SHA-256 `9b2fc7c4261485f9e1afffd78c3e944b956154df19c6926f7defebb7c5fdc0b7`) and its helpers from the original scratch directory. Set `EPDE_HISTORICAL_OPERATOR_DIR` to the directory containing those files on another installation. The experiment changes only the local add hook within each worker process. `paired_results.csv` gives per-search outcomes, and `shortlist.csv` gives all 24 counterfactual ranks. Every eligible source WAPE was reproduced from saved coefficients to less than 1.8e-15 absolute error.
