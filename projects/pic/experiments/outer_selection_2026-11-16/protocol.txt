Outer measurement validation and forced-KdV coefficient sensitivity
Frozen before new-seed outcomes, 2026-11-16.

Four systems: AC, forced KdV (kdv_cossin), wave, KS. Fit/inner-selection
records use fresh 5% Gaussian noise seeds 6/7/8; their paired independent
outer measurement records use seeds 9/10/11 respectively. Same clean fields
and sample grids: this tests new observation noise, not new trajectories.
Twelve system/seed runs, three rotated training groups per run. All registered
reference/equivalent equations are retained; no structural search/count.

Training uses one interleaved group. Inner selection uses the signed cross
residual from the other two groups. Outer evaluation uses those same two
group indices from the independent measurement record, which is generated
only AFTER all fits, weights and inner decisions are saved and hashed.
No refit after selection. No outer or clean result chooses a method.

Reuse EXACT native local/broad geometries from native_moments seed 3 (these
were coordinate/historical-geometry choices, not noise-dependent selections).
Fit native local, broad and stacked local+broad banks by corrected OLS and
unchanged 512-draw residual-covariance IRLS. Preserve full cross covariance.
No ridge, damping, penalties or new convergence thresholds. Preserve failures.
Do not silently average incomplete cases.

Two frozen selection rules, with the same choice set of native local IRLS
and native broad IRLS only:
  Primary: minimize native broad whitened signed inner cross residual.
  Comparator: minimize the previous interpolated broad whitened score.
Each selects per training fold, then averages the three outer fold scores.
If only one fit converges, select it and explicitly mark a forced choice.
If neither converges or the validation metric is infeasible, report failure.
Ties choose local. Mixed fits and OLS are fixed controls, not extra options
in this experiment. Fixed local/broad/mixed IRLS comparisons are primary;
OLS controls are reported separately. No reuse of old-seed baseline accuracy
as if it were a paired baseline for these new seeds.

Training pilots/noise estimates/512 shared operator draws remain unchanged
from the preceding estimator. Native validation uses 512 training draws;
the previous interpolated score uses the first 128. Both use training-only
pooled weights common to candidates/fits. Inner and outer raw operators
use each measurement group's own estimated noise for Gaussian contraction
correction. Outer noise estimates never reach fitting or selection.

After selection freeze, report outer signed scores for both metrics,
inner-to-outer score change, common frozen clean spectral residual energy,
coefficient errors, and clean versions of each validation metric. Assess
selection regret against the better available local/broad fit only as a
post-hoc diagnostic. Rotated folds share records; aggregate at system/seed
level and do not treat folds as independent replicates. Negative cross
scores are legitimate estimates, not truncated to zero.

For forced KdV, save training moment matrices and 512 covariance draws.
Compute fixed-weight linearized coefficient covariance including target/
feature noise cross terms, normalized weighted-design singular values and
weak coefficient directions. Additionally generate 64 independent pilot-
noise draws (separate RNG stream), refit both OLS and IRLS with the same
512 covariance draws, and report bootstrap failures, spread and drift
relative to each estimator's noiseless pilot fit. No bootstrap outcome
selects methods. These are conditional pilot/noise-model diagnostics;
they omit uncertainty in noise estimation and pilot reconstruction and
are not calibrated unconditional confidence intervals.

Checks: selection cannot access outer/clean metrics; disjoint group indices
and noise seed sets; deterministic tie/failure rules; native geometry
matches frozen inputs; constants preserved in moment matrices; baseline
helper invariants retained; complete result coverage; finite scored fits;
fresh noise replay; frozen selection replay; input/source/artifact hashes.
No candidate differential-equation solves, PINNs, production edits or commits.
