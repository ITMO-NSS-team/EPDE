Sampling-normalized dyadic coefficient contrasts
Frozen before outcomes, 2026-11-17.

All15 default systems, fresh anchor noise seeds21/22/23,5% noise, full
candidate panels. Reuse the established seed0 geometry and per-candidate
bank/OLS-or-CLSM choice, rebuilding anchor fits/corrections/validation
weights as in uncertainty_refit. Preserve constants and all equivalents.
No production edits, tuned gates/ridge, candidate ODE/PDE solves or PINNs.

Use exactly dyad's raw weak matrix, common widest-candidate slab floor,
dyadic partitions, all axes and stable minimum-norm slab solves. At every
partition contrast coefficients in sibling slabs (c0-c1,c2-c3,...). This
is a multiscale coefficient-change statistic, not an estimate divided by
the coefficient level. Coefficients are in their original feature units.

Estimate contrast noise from256 full-field bootstrap draws around the
standard reconstructed anchor, with its estimated AR noise. Shared field
draws preserve correlated/overlapping weak windows and cross-candidate
dependence. For each contrast estimate variance V and mean noise shift
b=mean(bootstrap contrast)-pilot contrast.

Compare primary Q_bias: mean[(observed contrast-b)^2/((1+1/256)*V)] within
each partition, then mean partitions and axes. The finite-Monte-Carlo factor
accounts for estimating the shift from256 draws. Control Q_raw omits b
and uses V. No coefficient-median denominator, no covariance ridge and
no covariance independence assumption: marginal standardization defines
the score; its joint distribution is calibrated empirically, not called
chi-square in general. A zero-variance zero contrast contributes0; a
nonzero contrast with exactly zero variance is recorded as infinite,
never clipped. Rank-deficient minimum-norm slab fits are recorded; these
contrasts do not establish identifiability of physical coefficients.

This standardized statistic is expected to remain noise-scale invariant
under a constant-coefficient null. Unlike relative MAD at a zero level,
its simple Gaussian null is finite and the same at zero/nonzero coefficient
levels. It is a change-versus-noise statistic, not a magnitude that must
converge to0 as observation noise decreases.

  D+Q_bias+complexity and D+dyad+complexity (three-objective controls).
