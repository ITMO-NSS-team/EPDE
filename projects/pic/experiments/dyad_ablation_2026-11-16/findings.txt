Direct dyad replacement check and joint objective uncertainty
2026-11-16

Decision: keep the current dyad criterion provisionally. None of the tested
replacements improves the primary matched structural screen. The next
problem is calibrating uncertainty and improving biased fits, not promoting
a new instability score on this evidence. The present bootstrap must not
be interpreted as a probability that an equation is correct.

1. Controlled replacement experiment

All 15 default systems, seeds 0/1/2 at 5% observation noise: 45 records and
60 equation/seed cases. These are REUSED full candidate panels. This is an
objective-only comparison, not a new evolutionary search or independent
confirmation. Candidates, main fitted coefficients, discrepancy scores,
constants, and registered equivalents remain identical between criteria.
All candidates share the original weak matrices and widest-candidate slab
floor. Primary discrepancy/fits: q0_white128/joint. Strict Pareto comparison
matches the historical screen; no tolerance or uncertainty penalty added.

2. Conditional uncertainty experiment

Twenty selected records: AC, forced KdV, KS and wave at all three seeds,
plus every other record where a nontrivial alternative changes primary
registered retention. These contain 25 equation/seed cases and 382
unregistered-competitor/reference pairs. Selection is diagnostic, not an
independent confirmatory sample.

Each record uses 128 parametric measurements around its reconstructed
pilot and estimated AR noise model, plus 128 fresh benchmark measurements
of the clean field at the actual 5% iid noise level: 5,120 measurements
total. The same field noise enters both objectives and all candidates.
Every dyad slab fit is recomputed. Main candidate coefficients, validation
weights, corrections, geometry and normalization remain fixed. This is
NOT uncertainty for the full discovery/fitting/selection pipeline.

For each pair, margins are competitor minus reference (negative favors
the competitor). Bootstrap errors subtract the noise-free pilot margin;
benchmark errors subtract the noise-free clean margin. Central 95%
bootstrap error ranges are checked against the actual benchmark errors.
Clean fields are diagnostic controls only; they select no estimator,
pilot, weight, metric or threshold.

SD ratio means bootstrap SD / benchmark SD. These are descriptive averages
of dependent pairs, not independent coverage trials or simultaneous
confidence guarantees. B=128 gives limited resolution, especially in tails.
Discrepancy error calibration is relatively good; individual systems/pairs
still deviate. The low mean dyad coverage and much higher median show
heterogeneous failures, rather than uniform undercoverage everywhere.

For the 19 previously closest dyad-eligible competitors, mean coverage is
93.75% for discrepancy, 82.03% for dyad, and 49.14% for prediction dyad.
Dyad coverage is 65.68% for added-term competitors, 86.47% for dropped-term
competitors, and 76.89% for reduced-form competitors. Weak extra terms are
a significant difficulty, but do not explain every failure.

Estimated noise sigma is within 0.944--1.043 times the known benchmark
sigma. Correct noise amplitude alone does not ensure correct uncertainty
for the nonlinear instability statistic.

3. What the failures mean

The dyad formula averages MAD(coefficient)/abs(median(coefficient)) over
partitions and axes. A nearly zero slab median can make the ratio very
large. Empirically, Lorenz seed0 e1:add_2 has median dyad 1.196 but maximum
13,096 across 128 actual repeated measurements. This is consistent with
ratio instability and makes SD estimates sensitive to rare samples.
The benchmark/bootstrapped dyad SD ratios vary greatly across pairs.
Replacing the ratio is not sufficient: prediction dyad has much less
extreme SD ratios yet worse error-range coverage and primary retention.

The reconstructed pilot also changes the objective being perturbed.
AC seed1's pilot field has only 1.47% relative L2 error, but its discrepancy
margin against drop_0 is -1.0891e-4, versus +2.3422e-5 on clean data. The
shift is 2.24 actual measurement SDs. Estimated discrepancy spread is
accurate here (SD ratio 1.059, error coverage 94.53%), yet raw bootstrap
Pareto retention is 6/128 versus actual repeated-noise retention 84/128.
Correctly estimating centered noise spread does not make raw bootstrap
win/retention frequencies reliable confidence statements.

For KS seed0, the pilot relative field error is 1.26%. Against add_7,
its discrepancy center is shifted by 5.60 actual SDs. Prediction-dyad
pilot and clean margins differ by 13.93 SDs. Its bootstrap error bias is
0.00275 versus actual noise bias 0.05420; coverage is 0/128 even though
the two SDs are close. For forced KdV seed0/drop_0 the analogous prediction
center shift is 20.11 SDs. These derivative-sensitive score differences
are not protected by a small field reconstruction error. More bootstrap
draws alone would not repair the missing bias.

Conditional retention under actual repeated measurements, averaged across
the 25 selected equation cases, is 80.94% for dyad versus 75.75% for
prediction dyad, with mean fronts 3.205 versus 3.723. These are repeated
measurement diagnostics of fixed fits, not new discovery success rates.
There are counterexamples: prediction dyad retains forced KdV seed2 in
128/128 versus dyad's 31/128, and forced KdV seed0 in 38/128 versus 7/128.
It still loses substantially on Burgers and several Lorenz cases. Thus
neither the original 56/60 nor these averages imply dyad is noise-robust
in every system. Dyad's primary forced KdV seed2 success is fragile.

Nominal conditional discrepancy intervals (observed margin minus bootstrap
error quantiles) include zero for AC seeds1/2 and KS seed0 among the 19
previously closest pairs. Example KS0: [-6.41e-6, 1.10e-5]. These are
diagnostics without a new pruning gate, simultaneous correction, or full
fit/selection uncertainty. The competitors were selected from observed data.

In contrast, AC seed0 remains negative against drop_0:
[-2.85e-4, -1.01e-4]. Forced KdV seeds0/1 remain strongly negative against
their reduced-form competitors: [-0.3672,-0.3392] and [-0.2507,-0.2306].
Their observed margins are approximately -52 actual measurement SDs.
The noiseless discrepancy with these frozen fits also favors these
competitors. This is not a close noise tie; uncertainty alone cannot fix
inaccurate reference coefficients or an insensitive discrepancy.

4. Consequences and next controlled work

Keep dyad as the structural coordinate and retain complexity as a serious
control. Report paired discrepancy uncertainty first as a diagnostic;
avoid turning uncalibrated dyad bootstrap frequencies into confidence
penalties or truth probabilities. Retain explicit constants and valid
ODE_simple phase/energy identities. Added near-zero terms and a genuine
nonzero constant are different situations; no constant ban is justified.

The next uncertainty experiment should separate two effects: pilot-induced
bias in the noise-free moment/instability centers, and uncertainty from
refitting the main coefficients. Use independent benchmark measurements
to test calibration of each change, then include geometry/noise-model
selection if making a full-pipeline claim. More draws may stabilize tail
estimates but cannot substitute for those checks. Persistent forced-KdV
fit/discrepancy bias remains a separate task. All these steps can remain
solver-free; this report does not claim they are already solved.

replay error is 1.1642e-10 (all relative/absolute tolerances pass). All
saved scores and measurement arrays are finite. Independent audit checks
1,440 original fronts, 1,146 pair/axis coverage calculations and 12,800
repeated-measurement fronts, plus raw data replay and source/input/array
hashes. Analytic ODE_simple identities and their constants pass dedicated
checks, as do prediction-metric unit/axis invariance and drift response.
An initial AC smoke attempt exposed JSON list versus tuple plan shapes;
the adapter was corrected before final results. No scientific arm changed.
