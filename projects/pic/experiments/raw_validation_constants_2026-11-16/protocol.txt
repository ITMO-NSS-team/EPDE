Frozen protocol: raw weak validation and explicit constant/intercept audit
2026-11-16

Primary test: evaluate EXISTING fixed coefficients, not refit or select them.
Start wave/KS x three seeds, also AC/forced KdV to diagnose previous failures.
If raw ranking separates accurate wave and KS options without an obvious
quadrature failure, expand unchanged to the core 15. Noise 5%, seeds 0/1/2.
Retain original candidate panel, registered equivalents, dyad, physical local
weak supports, training target-energy normalization and 3 raw time groups.
No candidate equation solves, PINNs, production changes or new search.

Each raw group is mapped to the original fine grid by a FIXED natural cubic
interpolant in time. There is no time smoothing or spatial filtering. The
existing weak bank's interior support excludes out-of-domain extrapolation;
assert this explicitly. Interpolation is not assumed exact: saved clean
subsamples undergo the identical map as a quadrature/discretization control,
compared against the original full clean weak bank after coefficients freeze.

The finite panel has polynomial degree <=3 in observed fields and their
linear derivatives. For fixed zero-mean joint Gaussian measurement noise:
  corrected(a b) = a b - C_ab
  corrected(a b c) = a b c - C_ab c - C_ac b - C_bc a.
Covariances propagate the existing per-group separable AR noise model through
the fixed time interpolant and the exact finite-difference operators used by
weak monomials. Different variables have independent errors in this model.
Coordinate and opaque known factors are deterministic. Constants and linear
monomials receive zero correction. These identities are exact under the
assumed known covariance; estimated covariance and real bias remain limits.

Primary discrepancy is the signed cross-product of corrected residual moments
from the OTHER TWO raw groups. Independent groups remove the squared-noise
floor; do not additionally subtract a within-group variance from that cross
product. Also save uncorrected raw cross-products and ordinary residual energy.
All six existing fitting options are ranked only as a validation diagnostic;
using these held-out scores to choose a fit would require another evaluation
split and is NOT reported as validated discovery.

Uncertainty: 128 Gaussian operator draws per raw group, centered around the
existing noisy-data reconstruction solely as a signal proxy for variance.
Apply the same analytical correction to every draw. 512 paired empirical
perturbations reuse group draw indices across candidates/options/rotations,
preserving correlation. No 1+1/B mean-correction factor: point correction is
analytic, not a bootstrap estimate. Report conditional dispersion and 64/128
covariance sensitivity; neither is calibrated physical-model confidence.

Constants/intercepts:
- Inventory physical constants in all registered structures; do not assume
  zero forcing as a general law, or ban offsets based on this catalog.
- Current panel fits only explicit terms and calls dyad with fit_intercept=False.
  Audit constant-column normalization; no hidden fitted intercept is added.
- Compare each reference with its existing reference-plus-constant candidate:
  changes in local discrepancy, dyad, all physical coefficients and clean fit.
- Decompose dyad into the explicit constant contribution and other terms.
  Re-rank a diagnostic counterfactual omitting ONLY the constant's dyad score;
  this models an unpenalized fitted intercept, not the primary objective.
- For fixed coefficients, set only the constant coefficient to zero and
  decompose raw cross discrepancy into its direct offset contribution and
  remaining contribution. Report residual-mean versus centered components.
  Centering is a diagnostic, not an authorized free offset in the objective.

Checks before outcomes: Gaussian polynomial identities by exact low-order
quadrature, covariance propagation against explicit dense small-grid maps,
constant weak column equals one, derivative of a constant vanishes, analytic
cross-noise cancellation, baseline coefficients/dyad/scores reproduce saved
values, common supports avoid extrapolation, clean coarse/full mismatch is
reported rather than hidden. Save hashes and all negative results.

Context source: Messenger and Bortz, Asymptotic consistency of the WSINDy
algorithm in the limit of continuum data, https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.16000 .
Its noise-induced spurious polynomial terms motivate checking lower-order
terms; our finite-sample correction/test is specified above, not established
as successful by that paper. No external claim replaces local validation.
