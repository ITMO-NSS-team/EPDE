Continuous weak fitting and operator-uncertainty evaluation
2026-11-16

Completed all 15 default systems, seeds 0/1/2, 5% noise: 45 system runs,
60 equation-seed cases. Every calculation uses saved fields, reconstruction,
weak integration, and linear algebra. No candidate ODE/PDE solves occur.

The useful change is continuous weak fitting on the previously selected
adaptive supports. The new bandwidth selection is not a consistent improvement.
The dyad axis remains fixed throughout, so these tests do not validate a new
instability objective. They isolate fitting and discrepancy changes.

Structure retention (at least one registered reference/equivalent survives
on the discrepancy/dyad Pareto front of the SAME finite candidate panel):

  Previous local fit + previous corrected cross-product             50/60
  Previous coarse-grid adaptive weak-fit experiment                 49/60
  Continuous weak CLSM fit + previous corrected cross-product       53/60
  Continuous weak CLSM fit + full continuous spectral score         53/60
  Continuous weak CLSM fit + energy-normalized operator-risk bank   52/60
  Continuous weak CLSM fit + natural-unit operator-risk bank        53/60

OLS on the continuous weak integrals also retains 53/60 with the full bank.
Ordinary residual energy with the new CLSM fit retains 47/60, versus 46/60
with the previous fit. The signed, mean-corrected cross-product remains more
useful than ordinary energy in this panel. Negative estimates are not clipped.

Per-system changes, old fit -> continuous fit, with the old score frozen:
  KS:             0/3 -> 2/3
  forced KdV:     0/3 -> 1/3
  NS (all slots): 8/9 -> 9/9
  Allen-Cahn:     2/3 -> 1/3
  Other slots: unchanged; nonlinear diffusion remains 1/3.
Thus four previously lost cases survive and one previously surviving case is
lost. This is a net gain of three, not improvement on every system.

Do not equate retention with recovery of accurate equations. The surviving
forced-KdV reference has poor coefficients and a favorable negative score.
Wave retains registered structures in all seeds while its fitted reference
becomes less accurate. These qualifications materially limit the 53/60 result.

Bandwidth result:
The natural-unit risk bank gains nonlinear-diffusion seed 2 but loses the
forced-KdV seed 1 survivor, leaving 53/60. The energy-normalized variant also
loses KS seed 2 and exchanges nonlinear-diffusion survivors, leaving 52/60.
The operator-risk rule therefore cannot be recommended as a universal fix.

Exact-coefficient controls expose the remaining discrepancy problem. For
forced KdV, the full continuous cross-products are +1996.4, +1458.8, -1337.7
across the three seeds, while clean residual energy is about 3.7e-7. The
natural-unit bank reduces them to +617.1, +916.7, +29.2, still far from zero.
These controls diagnose severe finite-record error; three seeds do not
separately identify bias and sampling variance. See failure_analysis.txt.

Validation and limits:
- All 45 result files passed coverage, source-hash, baseline-hash, and finite
  score checks. Frozen baseline reproduction max scaled error: 4.79e-10.
- Polynomial/modulated integration-by-parts max error: 4.15e-13.
- Exact finite-noise enumeration checks prefix-risk differences to 1.28e-15.
- Brute-force 2D prefix sums match; natural-unit selection is invariant under
  consistent rescaling of operator columns and units.
- Half/full fitting-bootstrap budgets give the same 60 binary retention
  outcomes in each primary arm. This holds the evaluation and selected banks
  fixed; it does NOT validate bank-selection or score uncertainty.
- Existing budgets retained: 128 for ac, kdv_cossin, pde_compound, pde_divide,
  ns; 32 for the other ten systems. Three rotations reuse each noisy record.
- Tests ran in two parallel processes, one BLAS thread each, below-normal
  priority. No production files were changed and no method was promoted.
- Fixed candidate panel, three noise seeds, one noise level; no new
  evolutionary search or independent-dataset generalization test.
- Risk normalizations are exploratory ablations, both reported. The initial
  four energy-only seed-0 probes are in results/; the full battery is in
  unit_results/. Do not mix the two directories.

Decision:
Keep the continuous weak-fit implementation as an experimental improvement,
especially for high derivatives. Preserve the old fit as a control because
wave and small-term cases regress. Do not promote the bandwidth rule or
claim that the discrepancy problem is solved. The next diagnostic should
measure uncertainty in PAIRWISE residual-score differences, with full
operator covariance and shared-data dependence, and check continuous-window
selection for wave/Allen-Cahn. No structural threshold is chosen here.
