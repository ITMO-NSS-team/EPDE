# Wave time windows and Allen–Cahn trajectory transfer

Use the twelve confirmation archives from the candidate-frequency study at 3,000 total fits. Keep discovered points, coefficients, training dyad, and discovery eligibility frozen. Score actual system handovers only. These experiments measure frozen candidates. They add no equation fits.

## Wave

Before inspecting held-out outcomes, use the original noisy training half to select a temporal radius. Compare the archived radius with every larger even radius that leaves at least four temporal window centres on the archived stride. Keep spatial windows, strides and test-function orders fixed. Estimate the field's iid noise standard deviation from the median absolute deviation of temporal second differences, divided by the Gaussian MAD constant and the square root of six. Propagate the estimated variance through the separable second-time-derivative window. Pick the shortest radius for which estimated noise variance is at most 10% of the measured weak derivative's mean square. If none qualifies, use the radius with the smallest fraction. Use the archive radius if the set of longer windows is empty.

Recompute held-out WAPE for every actual candidate and keep the archived training dyad. Compare native and selected radii using exactly six candidates per returned list, the previous median wave front size. Fill from successive Pareto fronts in order, breaking an oversized front by the sum of the two global objective ranks, then point ID. Also report all first-front recoveries and sizes. Keep native training eligibility and the held-out WAPE cutoff of one. Verify the original first front against the real archive. Report the registered physical coefficient's noisy and clean WAPE as a diagnostic only. Repeat on the original held-out field and 16 fresh held-out noise draws (927101–927116), shared across archives. The new draws vary measurements on the existing trajectory.

## Allen–Cahn

For each of the 11 archives with an assembled correct law, freeze the lowest-held-error eligible correct point and its previously observed lowest-error dominating front member. Separately compare the five same-target reaction-only rivals identified in the prior audit. Reproduce original errors before transfer. On the native periodic grid generate three new initial profiles with mode indices 1, 3 and 6. The initial displacement is 0.45 times sine mode k plus 0.20 times cosine mode k+1 with phase 0.3. Simulate the registered diffusivity 0.0001 and a matched zero-diffusivity control at each initial condition. Use exact reaction flow and Fourier diffusion splitting, verify convergence by halving the time step and doubling spatial resolution, and check the zero control against closed-form evolution. Use the original held-out spatial half and each archive's weak windows.

Evaluate each pair clean and with 16 fresh Gaussian noise draws (927201–927216). Within each profile, use the same noise arrays and absolute scale (5% of the positive field's standard deviation) for the positive and zero controls. Report the number of archive pairs in which the correct archived structure has lower median paired WAPE than the rival across the 16 draws. Report separately the direct reaction-only comparison and any cross-target pair. Noise draws and archived searches are repeated observations, not independent trajectories. Dyad remains a training diagnostic, so beating a rival on WAPE tests whether its original domination persists.
