Adding one unit of smoothness gives median CLSM energies 0.0003955 (AC),
0.01879 (wave), 0.06731 (forced KdV), and 28.381 (KS). It does not repair
the KS failure. Broad OLS improves wave even relative to the old pre-adaptive
wave fit (0.001334), but this cannot justify applying it to every system.

Forced-KdV residual energy decreases, yet coefficients remain far from the
known [-6,-1,1]. Several corrected broad Grams have negative eigenvalues.
Those are retained under the existing signed solve, not clipped to obtain
a preferred equation.

Why this test matters: whole-library numerical rank can be achieved in weak
moments that carry little reliable information about important coefficient
directions. Full-domain low-frequency projection discards useful KS detail.
Fitting therefore needs a measure of statistical information, not just the
number of moments or a floating-point rank check.

Selector constraint diagnosis: forced KdV has 12 library columns. On its
27 x 81 raw training grid, spatial radius 32 leaves one natural-stride spatial
center. Even the smallest allowed time radius 2 gives only 12 center rows,
which fails choose_window's requirement rows > library_columns + 1 (13).
Dense fitting strides do not remove its separate natural-stride score gate.
This excludes that broad radius before scoring. It does not by itself prove
why radius 4 beat radius 16 in the observed problematic fold.

The proposed next experiment selects among local/q-min broad/q+1 broad
fitting banks using the largest generalized noise-to-observed-energy ratio
over each candidate's feature directions. This uses training bootstrap
operators and no clean data, numerical-rank-only rule, or chosen SNR cutoff.
It will test whether a common data-driven rule can preserve KS information
while choosing a quieter wave representation. Its target-noise and common-
bias limitations must still be evaluated.
