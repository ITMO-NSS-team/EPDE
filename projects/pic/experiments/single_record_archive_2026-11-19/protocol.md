# Does an archive help with one observed trajectory?

Use wave and nonlinear diffusion, six paired fresh seeds (921101 through 921106), native WAPE and directional dyad, native weak least squares, fourth-order finite differences, ten NSGA-II epochs, seven terms maximum and plain mode. Generate a 5% noisy observation of each full benchmark once per seed, then split it before EPDE sees it. Each time half has the same number of rows. Omit the central observation between the halves. Preserve coordinates and grid spacing. The simulation noise amplitude uses the standard benchmark convention based on the full clean signal.

The primary experiment fits the early half and validates on the late half. A separate reverse-direction search checks sensitivity to which dynamics are observed. Each search sees only its discovery half for support generation, frequency estimation, weak-window selection, weights, source eligibility and dyad. Validation uses its own observed rows, keeps the training window footprint, and applies frozen coefficients at the original coordinates. Do not insert the reference or refit a candidate on the evaluation half.

Use held-out WAPE with the discovery dyad and the existing WAPE cutoff of one. Evaluate the existing blocked-WLS cutoff of one alongside the ungated result. The two internal block fits use discovery rows only and are evaluated on the corresponding opposite block of the held-out segment. Rank the returned front, the terminal population and all source-eligible scored candidates separately. Keep the complete nondominated front for each. Report both orientations of the split separately, exact support recovery in any target orientation, front sizes and failure stage. Fit a third search per system and seed on the complete record as the normal pipeline comparison. Its scores are in-sample. Each time-direction contributes six observations per system, without pooling the two directions as independent seeds.

As a diagnostic independent of ranking, fit only the physical reference support on the training weak rows and compare its held-out WAPE to the WAPE with the known coefficients. This distinguishes inaccurate weights or derivative measurements from failure to propose the support. These diagnostic fits cannot enter any candidate pool.
