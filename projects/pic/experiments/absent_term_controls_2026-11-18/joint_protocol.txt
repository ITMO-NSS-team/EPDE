Prospective shared-coefficient pilot for the forced KdV failure

Motivation: the refitted forced-only reference lost to absent additions in 243/256 new comparisons, whereas none beat the physical reference coefficients. Fix a two-trajectory sensitivity test before inspecting any of its outcomes. Use 16 fresh forced realizations (786001–786016) and 16 independently seeded unforced realizations (787001–787016) of the two registered trajectories. The physical KdV nonlinear and dispersive coefficients are shared in the registered equations and the known forcing is absent from the unforced input.

Leave the forcing coefficient and each tested extra term specific to the forced field. Thus each extension retains all its freedom to improve the forced score. Identical forced held-out groups, noise correction, whitening, normalization and discrepancy serve both arms. Report how often D(extended) is below D(reference) on the forced record, the three coefficient medians, and the fixed-truth score as a benchmark control. Verify reduction to the original fit when the second Gram is omitted. The known shared operators are a benchmark assumption. This tests repeated measurements on the same two underlying trajectories, not an unrestricted search or transfer to a third trajectory.
