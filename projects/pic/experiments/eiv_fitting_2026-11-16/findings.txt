Fresh-seed weak errors-in-variables coefficient experiment
Completed 2026-11-16. Solver-free, known-support fitting test.

The proposed raw weak residual-covariance IRLS estimator is a partial
improvement, not a general replacement. Broad windows improve AC, forced KdV
and wave median residual accuracy relative to the recomputed local CLSM
baseline, but seriously damage KS. Local windows preserve KS and fail to
deliver the wave/forced-KdV improvement. The scale tradeoff remains.

Twelve system runs completed: AC, forced KdV, wave and KS at 5% noise, fresh
seeds 3/4/5. These are new noise realizations on the existing four trajectories.
Geometry was fixed from the independent earlier seed-0 study. There are 12
reference-equation/seed cases; including four registered wave equivalents,
there are 24 registered-equation/seed cases, each with three training folds.
No candidate structure was selected, no dyad rule was changed, and no new
structural-retention or evolutionary-search result is claimed.

Method and controls

One interleaved raw group trains the coefficients, its noise model and its
covariance pilot; the other two groups provide final signed cross validation.
All three rotations are evaluated. No clean or held-out result selects a fit.
Raw fitting uses fixed natural-cubic interpolation and the existing analytic
Gaussian contraction correction for degree<=3 terms. Each constant keeps its
actual modulated weak column. Derivatives that remain on the data use the
fourth-order stencil; integration by parts and continuous weak quadrature
are otherwise used as in the earlier study.

For each training group, 512 joint operator draws model target/predictor and
row correlations. IRLS starts at corrected-raw OLS, computes the covariance
of delta_b-delta_A*c, then solves weighted least squares and repeats. The
pilot/noise model is fixed through iteration. Full covariance and design rank
are required at the frozen numerical tolerance; no ridge or null-direction
deletion is introduced. Convergence uses relative prediction change <=1e-8,
with a 100-iteration cap. Nonconverged fits are preserved as diagnostics and
excluded from complete three-fold averages.

Three geometries are reported: existing dense local windows, a local subset
with centers spaced at least one window radius, and minimum-q broad moments.
The local subset is determined only by radius/stride. For KS it equals the
original rows, so its identical results are not independent evidence.
Within each geometry compare uncorrected raw OLS, corrected raw OLS, raw
IRLS, reconstructed OLS and reconstructed CLSM. Reconstructed CLSM is refitted
on fresh data using the old system's 32/128 correction budget. The baseline
below is dense-local reconstructed CLSM, not the earlier joint selector.

Final validation uses the preceding common broad raw score: one pooled
training-only covariance from 128 operator draws is shared by every fitted
model. This final scoring covariance differs from the coefficient-dependent
covariance used inside IRLS. Clean fields enter only after fits/weights freeze.

Broad IRLS improves versus the baseline in 8/12 reference cases and worsens
in 4/12: AC improves 2/3, forced KdV 3/3, wave 3/3, and KS 0/3.
The four broad-IRLS medians alone must not be combined with local KS results
as though a validated data-driven selector had produced that combination.

Forced KdV improves beyond the broad corrected-OLS control on all three
seeds. Its broad-IRLS energies are 0.05958, 0.04853 and 0.01827, versus local
CLSM 0.20673, 0.16564 and 0.17017. Its median energy improves about 71.5%
relative to baseline and 37.1% relative to broad corrected OLS.
However, coefficients remain inaccurate: across nine folds, the medians for
[u*u_x, u_xxx, cos(t)sin(x)] are [-4.0959,-0.5199,0.4395], versus [-6,-1,1].
The nonlinear coefficient spans -9.3975 to +1.6829. This is partial recovery,
not a solved coefficient-identification problem.

For wave the large gain is mostly due to broad raw moments: corrected OLS
already has median energy 0.000794. IRLS gives 0.000780, improving on OLS in
two seeds but worsening in the third. Its coefficient range is 0.03852 to
0.04259 versus truth 0.04. Attributing the full baseline-to-IRLS gain to the
new iterative estimator would be incorrect.

AC broad IRLS improves median energy about 29.6%, but the diffusion estimate
is still unstable. Across nine folds it ranges from -0.0003261 to 0.0004832,
with median 0.00005735 versus truth 0.0001. Negative estimates are retained,
not clipped. The fit has not reliably recovered the small diffusion term.

Local IRLS is not a universal alternative. Dense-local median energies are
0.0002582 for AC and 0.3564 for wave, worse than baseline in all three seeds.
For forced KdV, each seed has at least one nonconverged dense-local fold;
we therefore do not report a median over only the successful folds. The
spaced-local variant also fails to converge in two forced-KdV folds.
KS local IRLS is accurate: median energy 4.86279e-5, improving versus baseline
in two seeds. But corrected raw local OLS is already 4.39112e-5 on median.
That evidence does not justify making IRLS mandatory for KS.

Newly isolated KS interpolation/representation problem

Clean fitting controls keep geometry and raw sampling fixed and replace the
noisy field by saved clean values, after all experimental fits are frozen.
Broad OLS on the full clean field has frozen residual energy 1.04687e-10.
Broad OLS after sampling the clean field at each group's time points and
applying the fixed interpolation has energy 0.212411. The corresponding
local interpolated-clean fit is 5.42760e-8. These are deterministic controls,
repeated identically across seeds, not extra independent observations.

The broad interpolated-clean KS coefficients are approximately
[-0.99974,-0.99612,-0.90226], [-0.99975,-0.99580,-0.89545], and
[-0.99975,-0.99591,-0.89724], versus [-1,-1,-1]. Small moment approximation
errors are amplified into a roughly 10% error in the fourth-derivative
coefficient. The actual broad-IRLS fourth-derivative estimates range from
-1.5134 to -0.6043 across noisy folds, while local fits remain accurate.

This isolates a weakness of the broad low-frequency fitting representation
under subsampling/interpolation. Noise-covariance reweighting alone does not
remove it. It is not evidence that the fourth-order stencil was accidentally
disabled. For forced KdV, broad interpolated-clean OLS energy is only
2.89772e-7; its large noisy-fit error has a different origin.

The common broad validation metric also has limits. For KS seed 3, broad
IRLS scores 3.598e-6 versus local IRLS 7.351e-6, even though their frozen
clean energies are 0.2440 versus 9.971e-5. On full clean data evaluated in
the broad validation representation, broad IRLS still wins (4.255e-6 versus
8.071e-6). Thus this particular ranking failure is not merely stochastic
validation noise. A few broad moments can miss an error exposed by the
richer frozen clean bank. Do not select fits with that score alone.

Convergence and controls

There are 216 IRLS fits including registered wave equivalents: 210 converge,
six do not meet the frozen 100-step criterion. Five failures are forced KdV
(three dense, two spaced); one is a spaced-local wave equivalent. Four of
the KdV failures show an alternating two-cycle: the two-step coefficient
change is tiny while the one-step change remains substantial. One KdV fit
just misses tolerance (1.0454e-8 versus 1e-8); the wave equivalent is still
changing. All broad IRLS fits converge. No covariance-rank failure occurs.
This distinguishes optimizer convergence from coefficient accuracy.

Known-covariance GLS, joint covariance algebra including target/predictor
cross terms, feature-unit scaling, constant preservation and explicit
zero-covariance rejection pass. The ODE_simple energy and phase identities
are preserved by clean-fit controls with noise-derived IRLS weights. Their
constants are 2.68999715, 0.99999947 and -1.29999931 in the existing equation
orientations; differences from 2.69, 1, -1.3 are the source stencil error.
The identities require derivative order at most one, and are not removed.

All source and input hashes, fresh raw records, finite scored fits, iteration
criteria and aggregate means are checked by audit.py. See completion_audit.json.
The first invocation of the reporting-only diagnose.py hit an import-name
collision with an older summarize.py. Its CSV helper was made local and the
report rerun. No fitting code, result, criterion or seed was changed or rerun.

Interpretation and next experiment

The estimator helps on broad moments, particularly forced KdV, but cannot
serve as a universal coefficient fitter. The clean KS control makes the
next issue concrete: address approximation-error amplification and retain
informative local moments during both fitting and validation. A focused
next test should compare native-sample weak quadrature and mixed local/broad
constraints, including their cross covariance, against the present controls.
That test has NOT been performed here. Native sampling alone is not assumed
to recover information lost by subsampling. Damping could address observed
IRLS cycles, but convergence would not by itself fix the inaccurate fits.
