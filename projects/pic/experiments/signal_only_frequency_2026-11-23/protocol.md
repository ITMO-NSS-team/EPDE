# Signal-only frequency proposals

Compare two lists of at most two angular frequencies on each displacement training record. The baseline is EPDE's mean-centred Hann spectrum with its native peak interpolation and merging. The experimental rule keeps the strongest Fourier peak. If a second peak has at least one tenth of its energy, keep that peak too. Otherwise try a period estimated from repeating pieces of the trace. Search lags from 75% to 125% of the first spectral period, retaining at least one third of the trace in each overlap. Smooth at one thirty-second of that period and use overlap-centred, variance-normalised correlation. Require an interior local maximum of at least 0.8. When the record spans at least four estimated periods, use its trailing half to reduce the influence of the initial transient. Use the resulting estimate for the second slot if it differs from the first by at least 0.5%. Fall back to the native list otherwise.

The extractor receives displacement and sample spacing. It has no access to equation terms, fitted coefficients, initial state, registered frequency, trajectory name or held-out observations. The two candidates may be alternative estimates of the same rhythm. The scorer uses the registered frequency only after both lists are returned.

Evaluate the stored Duffing and forced-oscillator records on their original training halves, with 250 paired fresh 5% observation-noise draws (seeds 926001–926250). Count lists with any candidate within 1% of the registered value. Also report nearest relative error and paired gains and losses. Reproduce the archived native peaks on the 12 prior confirmation seeds before scoring the fresh draws.

In a separate simulation, vary the initial conditions and driving frequency, so a method can fail by following the system's natural oscillation. Use initial states (1,0) and (0,1) at the catalog frequency, and a third trajectory at 1.2 times the catalog frequency from (1,0), for each oscillator. Also simulate the original starting states for the duration comparison. Compare training durations of 1, 4 and 8 times the stored training duration, with unchanged sampling spacing. Use 50 fresh paired noise draws per trajectory (926501–926550). Keep the absolute noise scale fixed at 5% of the standard deviation of the first training prefix and reuse noise at shared times. Validate numerical convergence. Report trajectory-specific outcomes. These simulations use independent generated observations and never extend the archived training split using its held-out data.
