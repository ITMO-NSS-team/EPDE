Local weak discrepancy and continuous raw fitting: core-15 results
2026-11-16; 45 runs, 60 equation-seed cases; 5% noise, seeds 0/1/2.
Solver-free. All candidates, dyad values and physical supports held fixed.

Local weak evaluation is a useful improvement for nonlinear diffusion and
reduces high-derivative evaluation noise substantially. It is not a complete
fix. Fitting directly on raw observations is not a reliable replacement for
the reconstructed-field fit, especially for wave.

Retained registered structures / 60 (finite-panel Pareto survival):
  Old fit, old spectral cross-score                 50
  Continuous reconstructed fit, spectral score     53
  Continuous reconstructed fit, LOCAL score        54
  Continuous RAW CLSM fit, spectral score           51
  Continuous RAW CLSM fit, LOCAL score              54
  Continuous RAW OLS fit, LOCAL score               54

With the continuous reconstructed fit, ordinary local residual energy retains
53/60 versus 47/60 with ordinary spectral energy. Mean-corrected and
uncorrected local cross-products both retain 54/60. Improvement is primarily
the weak test representation here, not a universally necessary correction.

Score-only comparison, identical fitted coefficients:
  Nonlinear diffusion: 1/3 -> 3/3.
  Forced KdV:          1/3 -> 0/3.
  All other slots retain the same binary outcomes.
The lost forced-KdV survivor had badly biased coefficients and a favorable
negative spectral score; its earlier survival was not accurate recovery.
The local score reveals competitors that truly beat that fit on clean data.

Why nonlinear diffusion improves:
At seed 1, dropping (u_x)^2 changed the spectral discrepancy by -2.665 with
paired SD 7.304, although the clean spectral gap was +0.1465. The local bank
gives gap +0.2164 with SD 0.008422 and clean local gap +0.2023. The required
term now wins clearly, without changing coefficients or using clean data
to choose the bank. Seed 2 is also recovered.

Exact-law discrepancy standard deviations, spectral -> local:
  Nonlinear diffusion seed 0: 10.6723 -> 0.010668
  Nonlinear diffusion seed 1: 11.0233 -> 0.009000
  Nonlinear diffusion seed 2: 13.0508 -> 0.007723
  KS seed 0:                  0.15266 -> 0.00006455
  KS seed 1:                  0.15001 -> 0.00004904
  KS seed 2:                  0.16059 -> 0.00005528
The banks and their training-energy normalizations differ; these are not
two estimators of exactly the same norm. Clean discrimination between
candidate equations must be checked alongside the smaller score noise.

Remaining problems:
- KS seed 0 still loses to small added-term candidates. Added u^2 improves
  the local score by 7.91e-7 with paired SD 2.14e-6, yet worsens clean local
  score by 9.97e-7. This remains a very small, unresolved ranking reversal.
- Allen-Cahn seeds 0/2 still lose small diffusion contributions. Some dropped
  models also beat the currently fitted reference on clean data, so score
  precision alone cannot repair those coefficient estimates.
- All forced-KdV seeds fail with the reconstructed fit. Seed 2's training
  fold 0 inherited spatial radius 4, q 4 from the earlier selector. Its exact-law
  local score is 14142, overwhelming the other folds (-4.51 and +10.18).
  The local bank is only as good as its supports; reusing a tiny support can
  retain severe derivative noise. No hand-chosen radius was substituted.
- Wave retains equivalent/reference structures but fitted-reference accuracy
  remains poor. Continuous raw fitting worsens the median frozen clean energy
  from 0.07763 to 0.90982 (old fit: 0.001334). Do not interpret its 3/3 retained
  structures as accurate coefficient estimation.

Raw-fitting tradeoffs:
Median frozen clean energy improves modestly for KdV and NS continuity but
worsens for nonlinear diffusion, variable-coefficient PDE and wave. Allen-Cahn
retention falls from 1/3 to 0/3; forced KdV gains a survivor but still has poor
coefficients. Its aggregate 54/60 does not justify replacing the current fit.
Half/full raw CLSM budgets retain 54/60 locally, but individual fit/score
behavior and global-score counts differ. Full data are retained in the CSVs.

Validation: all 45 input/source hashes verified, all scores finite, old
50/60 and current 53/60 spectral baselines reproduced. Continuous quadrature
and paired-noise code use the previously validated implementations. No
production files were changed. No clean fields enter tuning or fitting.

These tests reuse the same three noisy records per system and finite panel.
Conditional uncertainty holds coefficients/denominators/bank choices fixed
and excludes common reconstruction bias. No uncertainty threshold modifies
dominance. Neither 54/60 nor the apparent error ranges are full-search or
independent-dataset validation.

Next targeted experiment: use independently reconstructed training replicas
in weak coefficient regression (symmetric instrumental cross-products),
comparing uncorrected and mean-corrected operators. Keep outer held-out groups
and local scoring separate. This tests a mechanism for errors-in-variables
without candidate integration or choosing windows from known coefficients.
