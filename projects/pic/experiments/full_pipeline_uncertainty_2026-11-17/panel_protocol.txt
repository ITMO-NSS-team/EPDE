Exploratory fixed-panel calibration from saved primary draws
===========================================================
Proposed after inspecting the initial primary coverage/geometry results.
This is a post hoc method-development comparison, not fresh confirmation.
Do not alter or replace any primary result or select cutoffs using truth.

Keep the observed field, pilot, noise fits, bootstrap fields, centering and
covariance matrices exactly as archived. For each bootstrap error Gram and
covariance, restrict the generalized operator maximum to each distinct
stochastic support (target plus predictors) in the pre-existing candidate
panel. Take the maximum over ALL those supports and BOTH weak banks. Use the
same 123rd-of-128 cutoff rule. Both fixed- and refitted-pilot modes are kept.
This gives a uniform-error target over the union of the fixed candidate
subspaces, not all coefficient combinations in the full library. Constants
are excluded only from stochastic whitening and remain in every candidate.

Freeze the panel cutoff/regions before evaluating its observed error statistic.
Report coverage on the same 69 observed datasets as an exploratory comparison,
including any losses, geometry and width changes. It does not account for an
adaptive search outside this fixed panel. Independent outer replication is
required before treating an apparent improvement as confirmed.
