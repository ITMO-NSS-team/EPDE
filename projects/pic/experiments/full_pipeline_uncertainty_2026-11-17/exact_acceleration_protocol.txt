Exact-algebra runtime work; statistical specification remains frozen
===================================================================
The full KS procedure is expensive. Test two computational identities without
changing MC16, bootstrap128, estimators, reconstruction, streams or cutoffs.

1. For temporal contrasts D with n rows and r>2n spatial replicates, thin QR of
D'=Q R gives D D'=R'R. Replace D by sqrt(n/r)*R' and use count n*n during the
normalized Gaussian profile optimization. Both q/count and logdet/count match
the original n*r profile algebraically, including normalized derivatives.
Four starts/bounds/optimizer tolerances are unchanged. Final state-score
certification, any polishing and sigma profiling use the ORIGINAL contrasts.
This is sufficient-statistic compression, not frequency removal or subsampling.

2. For polynomial weak terms through degree3, corrected moment error around mu
equals the sum of pilot products times Wick noise polynomials. E.g.
  (mu+e)^2-v-mu^2 = 2mu*e+(e^2-v),
  (mu+e)^3-3v(mu+e)-mu^3 = 3mu^2*e+3mu*(e^2-v)+(e^3-3v*e).
Mixed factors use the corresponding pair covariance. Compute shared noise and
linear terms once for both pilot choices. The same16 realizations and exact weak
kernels are retained. Constants have exact zero error. Verify against the
uncompressed likelihood and original per-pilot metric before use. Preserve the
original implementation and unfinished execution records if a faster variant
is deployed; no result is selected or discarded based on coverage.
