Reconstruction replay and archival correction, before primary coverage
=====================================================================
Cold-process recomputation of the correlation-aware pilot produced two close
floating-point outputs on each KS anchor. Across12 fresh processes, maximum
absolute difference was2.22e-10, maximum relative difference7.43e-11. Ordinary
pilots remained bitwise identical. The specific low-level source is not pinned
down; this is observed numerical reconstruction variation, not a demonstrated
change in the statistical method. Initial exact-hash audit failures are retained.

Every original correlated pilot hash was reproduced, and matching arrays are
saved in pilot_replays. Factorial audit uses these exact arrays for replaying
bootstrap noise and fits, while independently comparing reconstruction output
against them with a maximum-absolute bound1e-9 times maximum field magnitude.
Original draw and pilot hashes remain exact; no statistical test cutoff changes.

Primary source variant *_saved adds archival arrays: the anchor pilot and first/
last refitted bootstrap pilots. All other fields are reproducible from stored
anchor model, pilot and random seed. Original source/preflight/checks remain.
The estimator, reconstruction, weak moments, covariance metric, MC16, bootstrap
128, streams and coverage decisions are unchanged. This is not an estimator
selection based on coverage. Preflight and numerical comparisons precede fresh
anchors51..53. Recomputed pilots are tested numerically; saved inputs and draws
are always checked by exact hashes.
