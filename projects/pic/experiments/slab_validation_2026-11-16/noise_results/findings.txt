Noise-support follow-up, 2026-11-16

Outcome
The Cauchy carrier's low discrepancy is reproducible on pure noise. Kepler has
a different failure: accurate-looking field estimates still distort the
inverse-power balance. These results support investigating discrepancy and
the construction of its columns while retaining production dyad. They do not
establish a replacement objective or justify a universal signal/noise gate.

Protocol
64 independent artificial noise draws per observed record, three noise seeds
for rational, Kepler and Cauchy (576 unique draws). Both rational archive arms
use the same draws. Every archived structure is evaluated, 67 including the
four references. The existing AR(1) model estimates sigma and rho from each
observed field. Draws are added to its data-only GCV estimate; derivatives,
inverse tokens and products are rebuilt jointly, with and without rerunning
GCV. Frequencies, weak windows and fitted equation coefficients are frozen
within each comparison. No new search, objective modification, or pruning.

The covariance preserves common measurement noise across columns. It is an
average of pointwise replicate covariances, not an independent-row model or
a confidence test. Pure-noise controls contain no estimated or true signal.
Known clean fields enter only the separately labelled oracle diagnostics.

Noise D is the median over 64 draws with the observed raw coefficients frozen.
It is not a refitted residual. Archive_0 has the form

    u'' sin(omega t) = c u' cos(omega t).

For the nested central-fourth-order stencil, the Fourier derivative response is

    omega_FD = (8 sin(omega h) - sin(2 omega h)) / (6 h).

At seed 0, omega=80.541782 and omega_FD=69.369714, while c=-69.387576.
The agreement across all seeds, low pure-noise residuals, and nearly perfectly
correlated propagated columns support a derivative/window-induced relation.
This is an approximate filtered relation, not an exact analytical law of u.
The observed seed-0 smoothed carrier has weak WAPE 0.005563 but pointwise WAPE
40.2957. This pointwise diagnostic is not proposed as a noise-robust objective.
Clean frozen-coefficient WAPE also has a very small target denominator; it
must not be interpreted as a large absolute physical error by itself.

RMS estimated signal/noise for the raw carrier target is 0.000623, 0.002466,
0.003899. After the smoothing pipeline it is 0.624, 1.007, 1.553. Thus even
these diagnostic estimates do not consistently put the surviving smoothed
carrier below a ratio of one. Shared-noise cancellation alone is not a
universal invalidity test: real equations can also cancel correlated errors.

Kepler: inverse-power balance remains inaccurate
The reference and leading competitor have strongly supported weak columns.
Their target signal/noise ratios are about 200-260; reference inverse-column
ratios are about 78-170. A low-signal-column exclusion cannot separate them.

With analytical reference coefficients (-1, 0.6), the smoothed reference's
target errors are 0.538%, 0.470%, 0.491%, while combined RHS errors are 6.425%,
7.107%, 9.606%. These are RMS errors normalized by clean target RMS. The
oracle audit therefore locates most error in -u^-2 + 0.6 u^-3. Small field or
individual-column errors need not imply an accurate difference of those terms.
The analytical law's smoothed WAPE is 0.04863, 0.05426, 0.07262. Refitting
partly absorbs these distortions, but the true structure remains dominated
in the previously saved comparison.

Interpretation and next experiment
Keep dyad unchanged; it measures coefficient constancy and can consistently
rate a preprocessing-induced relation as stable. Do not turn covariance or
noise-support estimates into a production penalty from this experiment.

The next useful falsification test is a common observation-space discrepancy
for a small set of finalists: fit coefficients and any initial state using a
training part of the same record, then compare predicted u on held-out parts.
Hold preprocessing and initial-state inference inside training data; keep all
time/trig terms. For these ODEs use converged numerical solves and report
singular/failed equations explicitly. This is an untested proposal, not a
claim that it will resolve Kepler or generalize directly to PDE candidates.
The earlier PINN-loss issue in project_solver_discrepancy_broken.md makes
solver convergence an essential check before interpreting prediction error.

Validation
Observed raw/smoothed scores reconstructed for all archived structures.
Joint covariance projection agrees with direct residual perturbations.
Linear Cauchy columns obey F(signal+noise)-F(signal)=F(noise) numerically.
Full 64-draw run completed successfully; plot inspected. Production code was
not edited. The parametric bootstrap remains conditional on the signal/noise
estimates and on frequencies selected from the original observed record;
it does not account for frequency-selection uncertainty or provide new data.
