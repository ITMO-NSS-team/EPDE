Independent residual cross-products: Kepler feasibility result
2026-11-16

Conclusion
----------
The cross-product gives a meaningful improvement in point rankings, but the
remaining score differences are not reliably resolved. The initial GCV-based
protocol restores reference nondominance in two of three seeds. An exploratory
exact-leave-one-out bandwidth variant restores it in all three seeds against
the four archived rival families. However, the score differences for A, B and D
have broad conditional uncertainty ranges spanning zero in every seed. The
smallest point margin also remains sensitive to Monte Carlo bias estimation.
This is not a validated production selector or a full-search recovery result.

Scope
-----
Five structures: reference plus one representative of each of A, B, C and D.
Three existing noise seeds, each using one record split by sample index modulo
three. All three choices of training group were evaluated. The analysis does
not cover every other archived candidate or a new evolutionary search.

No candidate ODE/PDE was integrated. No new experimental record was introduced.
No production objective, dyad implementation, or keep rule was changed.

Method
------
One raw group selects the existing two token-frequency slots and spectral
bandwidth, then fits coefficients with CLSM on its own GCV reconstruction.
The other groups independently reconstruct the signal and rebuild all terms.
Every evaluation-group smoother/noise model sees only that group's raw data.

Evaluate complete residuals r_A and r_B, then score
  D_product = Re sum(conj(r_A)*r_B) / S_train,
where S_train is target energy in the training reconstruction. The matched
ordinary control is
  D_energy = (sum|r_A|^2 + sum|r_B|^2)/(2*S_train).
Use the same test bank, weights, coefficients and normalization in both.
For the spectral bank, positive/negative conjugate frequencies count equally.
Average the normalized scores across the three training rotations. They share
observations and are not treated as independent statistical replicates.

The mean-corrected arm subtracts E[F(smooth(pilot+noise))]-F(pilot) estimated
separately in each evaluation group. It targets nonlinear and smoothing bias.
Both score arms use the same corrected training coefficients. No residual
variance enters the denominator. Signed products are retained without clipping
or square roots; a negative value is not a physically negative model error.

The original full-record raw CLSM dyad coordinates are frozen to isolate the
discrepancy axis. New training-group dyad values are logged separately. Thus
this is a discrepancy feasibility comparison, not an entirely new validated
objective pair. Supports were previously selected on the full record.

Why the bandwidth follow-up was needed
-------------------------------------
The initial seed-1/group-0 GCV projection selected mode 498 for 500 training
observations (499 degrees of freedom). Exact leave-one-out error for the SAME
DCT projection family selected mode 82. The formula was checked against actual
omitted-observation refits. This selection uses training observations only.

GCV maximum modes by seed, training groups 0/1/2:
  seed 0: 83 / 99 / 81
  seed 1: 498 / 85 / 93
  seed 2: 82 / 93 / 71
Exact-LOO maximum modes:
  seed 0: 83 / 99 / 82
  seed 1: 82 / 85 / 93
  seed 2: 89 / 93 / 71

The exact-LOO rule was applied to all training groups, not just the failing one.
It was added AFTER inspecting the GCV failure and is an exploratory result;
the original result is preserved. No favorable bandwidth was selected using
the true equation's ranking.

Uncertainty and the extended Monte Carlo check
---------------------------------------------
Conditional uncertainty propagates independent centered bootstrap deviations
in the two evaluation groups while keeping training fits, frequencies, bank
and bias estimates fixed. Reuse of evaluation groups across rotations is
preserved. Quantiles are descriptive conditional spreads, not calibrated full
confidence intervals; omitted training/selection uncertainty remains.

All A/B/D-minus-reference 5--95% ranges span zero in every seed. Family C is
clearly separated in this diagnostic. For example, in seed 1 the small A gap
is overwhelmed by the conditional spread.

Because seed 1 had the smallest exact-LOO margin, extend its three independent
bootstrap streams from 128 to 256 draws without replacing prior draws:
  draws        A-minus-reference       D-minus-reference
    64            0.0002520                 0.0002808
   128            0.0001417                 0.0001726
   256            0.0000633                 0.0000960

At 256 draws reference D=0.00170483 and A D=0.00176814. The A-minus-reference
conditional 5--95% range is [-0.009012, 0.008830]. The point margin remains
positive but is much smaller than both the sampling spread and its recent
Monte Carlo change. We therefore do NOT claim a numerically well-separated
or statistically established victory, despite zero point dominators.
More bootstrap draws cannot remove the original record's measurement noise.

Validation
----------
Independent-noise population control: true squared error 0.25, ordinary energy
7.43, cross-product 0.25. Known mean-shift control returns 0.25 after
correction. Exact-LOO versus brute refits agrees within 2.42e-15.
Split coverage/disjointness, A/B symmetry, identical-group identity, and
energy-product=half squared residual difference all pass. Weak integration
and complex-intercept handling reuse the validated spectral implementation.
Clean controls and source/draw digests are saved with each result.

Settings and limitations
------------------------
Three groups are the minimum for separate training and two evaluations; all
three rotations are used. Smoothing, frequencies, noise models and bandwidth
are data-driven. Boundary support comes from the groups' common interpolation
domain. Bump order/local window/old dyad are retained experimental controls.
Bootstrap count is a computation budget with explicit numerical sensitivity,
not a ranking threshold. 5/50/95% summaries are not acceptance gates.

The independence argument assumes iid raw measurement noise. Bootstrap mean
correction is a plug-in approximation, not proof of unbiased weak quantities.
The training signal scale is estimated. Candidate selection on the full record
and searching many noisy signed scores can introduce further selection bias.
These results support further investigation of discrepancy estimation, while
the unresolved variance prevents a reliable production ranking claim.
