Actual noise-aware estimator test, 2026-11-16

This is the implemented/tested estimator proposed after the oracle objective
swap. The earlier clean-data swap was only a diagnostic and was not a test
of noise-aware fitting. User constraint remains solver-free.

Verdict
Mean-plus-covariance corrected regression does NOT resolve Kepler. There are
still 10 reference-dominating archived equations in every noise seed, with
either ordinary or crossed discrepancy, on both raw and smoothed data.
Coefficient estimates can improve substantially without fixing structural
ranking. The method should not become the default on these results.

What was tested
- Four existing archives (67 structures including references), three noisy
  records per system, raw and GCV-smoothed preprocessing, 5% measurement noise.
- 64 parametric-bootstrap draws for each noisy record and each separate clean
  control. Nine unique noisy records plus three clean controls = 768 draws.
  Rational archive arms reuse the same generated fields.
- Noise model estimated from the current observed record. Bootstrap centred
  on its data-only GCV pilot; all nonlinear tokens/derivatives rebuilt jointly.
- Production OLS, covariance-only corrected moments (CLS), and mean-plus-
  covariance corrected moments (CLSM).
- Every corrected method fits the FULL record AND EVERY dyad slab using the
  same estimator. Production partitions and MAD/abs(median) aggregation are
  retained. Each archive has only one admissible two-slab partition.
- Ordinary, within-reconstruction, and crossed discrepancies are evaluated
  on the original or split-reconstructed weak columns. Noise variance is
  never used as a discrepancy denominator.
- Coefficients and dyad are recomputed; structures, intercept presence, weak
  windows, frequencies and common comparison rows stay fixed. No pruning,
  evolutionary search, ODE/PDE solving, or production-code edit.

CLSM construction
Let F be the weak-column construction and s the GCV pilot. The raw bootstrap
is F(s+e), the smoothed bootstrap F(GCV(s+e)). Both use F(s) as their pilot
reference; this includes smoothing bias in the latter mean-shift estimate.
Estimate mean shift b and row-wise joint covariance C from the draws.

Z = observed weak columns - b
G = Z_X' Z_X - (1+1/B) sum_rows C_XX
g = Z_X' Z_y - (1+1/B) sum_rows C_Xy
Fit G c = g. The intercept column has zero noise correction.

CLS omits the mean shift and uses factor 1 instead of 1+1/B. Signed symmetric
moment systems are solved with the production dyad numerical rank tolerance
(1e-12), scaled by observed column norms. Negative eigen-directions are NOT
clipped, and no ridge, OLS fallback or candidate exclusion is used.

Smoothed-data fits
Rational and the historical capped/no-trig control are 0/0/0 under OLS and
CLSM for both discrepancies. Kepler changes from 10/11/10 to 10/10/10 for
both discrepancies. Cauchy remains 1/1/1 with ordinary discrepancy and 0/0/0
with crossed discrepancy, regardless of estimator.

Concrete Kepler example
Seed 1 raw reference coefficients:
  analytical: [-1, 0.6]
  OLS:        [-0.99162428, 0.59318550]
  CLSM:       [-0.99904614, 0.60001208]
Despite this improvement, 10 archived equations still dominate the reference.
The correction is applied to coefficient estimation; the observed residual
columns still contain their measurement and reconstruction errors.

Controls and numerical limitations
- All clean controls preserve reference nondominance, for every estimator
  and both discrepancy choices. Noise estimates from clean data are around
  1e-14 or smaller; clean truth is never supplied to the noisy estimator.
- Every saved OLS objective is reproduced before comparison.
- Zero-correction numerical control: maximum absolute D difference 8.51e-13,
  maximum absolute I difference 1.51e-4; no dominance comparisons change.
- Exact synthetic population checks recover slope 2 and intercept 1 with
  correlated noise in both target and feature, and with a known mean shift.
- Repeating CLSM with each 32-draw half preserves all crossed-discrepancy
  dominance sets. Kepler's ordinary counts also stay 10/10/10. Rational's
  ordinary counts vary between zero and one, so those marginal comparisons
  depend on bootstrap sampling.
- 507 of 1608 CLSM full/slab systems in the main 64-draw runs are indefinite
  (the count includes noisy and clean controls). Such roots are stationary
  moment-equation solutions, not minima of a positive least-squares objective.
  They are retained and flagged, not silently repaired or dropped. This is
  an additional reason not to treat the correction as a production solution.

Scope
This is a conditional-bootstrap, fixed-structure experiment, not independent
validation. The pilot and original coefficients/frequencies use the same
record. Finite-bootstrap bias-variance correction is approximate. Shared
errors across columns are modelled; no independent-weak-row significance
claim is made. No conclusion here establishes failure of all errors-in-
variables estimators or identifies an unavoidable one-record information limit.
