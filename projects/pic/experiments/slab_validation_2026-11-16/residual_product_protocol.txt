Residual cross-product feasibility test, before numerical ranking
2026-11-16

Use the existing three Kepler noisy records and four distinct archived rival
families. Split raw observations by index modulo three. Every group spans the
record but has disjoint raw noise. Rotate the training group through all three.

Each training group independently selects the two existing token-frequency
slots from its raw spectrum and the spectral-test bandwidth by raw-u DCT GCV.
Fit the same archived supports by CLSM on the training group's GCV spline,
reconstructed on the original grid. Bootstrap training-group noise through
the same smoother and all nonlinear features for the CLSM correction.

Each evaluation group has its own GCV spline, noise estimate, independent RNG,
and bootstrap reconstructions. Neither evaluation group's observations select
the coefficients, frequencies, bandwidth, or normalization. Their full weak
residuals are compared by a signed cross-product. A matched squared-energy
control uses exactly the same coefficients and fields. Mean-corrected and
uncorrected residual arms use the same corrected training coefficients.

Primary score: real sum(conj(r_A)*r_B), normalized by the training spline's
target energy, using the preceding full-record oscillating weak bank. Also
test the original local zero-modulation weak bank. Frequencies have equal
weights (negative conjugates count equally); no inverse-noise weighting.
Average normalized scores over the three training rotations. Keep the saved
full-record raw CLSM dyad coordinate fixed for the primary Pareto comparison.
Training dyad fits are logged separately.

Subtract the bootstrap estimate E[F(smooth(pilot+noise))]-F(pilot) from each
evaluation group's weak columns. This includes the plug-in estimate of
nonlinear/smoothing bias. No clean value is used for this correction.

Clean controls use the saved clean record only after the noisy bank is fixed.
Also report a clean-field swap retaining noisy-trained coefficients. Neither
control selects parameters or ranks noisy candidates.

Uncertainty: propagate centered, independently generated evaluation-group
bootstrap deviations through the product, preserving shared groups across
rotations. Report paired score-difference quantiles and fold variation. These
are conditional spreads, not full calibrated confidence intervals; training
fit/frequency/bandwidth uncertainty is excluded. Do not treat rotations as
independent observations or clip negative estimates.

Settings audit:
- Group count three: minimum for disjoint training and two evaluations.
- Three rotations: exhaustive choices of training group.
- Smoothing, token frequencies, spectral bandwidth, and noise model: data-driven.
- Support trimming: common interpolation domain of the three groups.
- Weak bump order/local window/old dyad: retained controls from prior experiment.
- Draw budget initially 128: computational budget, not a statistical cutoff.
  Report results at 32/64/128; extend if changes affect interpretation.
- Bootstrap RNG seeds and Fourier lattice: reproducibility/numerical design.
- Numerical rank tolerance: existing production solver convention.
- 5/50/95% summaries: descriptive uncertainty, no acceptance gate.

Validate independent-noise population identity, known-bias correction,
disjoint split coverage, swapped-group symmetry, identical-group equivalence,
and energy-product=half squared difference. No solver or production edits.
