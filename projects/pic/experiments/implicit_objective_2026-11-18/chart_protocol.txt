The second chooses its internal target from the data by best conditioning: maximize the smallest singular value of the standardized nonpivot columns, breaking exact ties by canonical global term index, then use that chart's least-squares relation. The third uses every possible target, normalizes each fitted coefficient vector in the same standardized coordinates, averages their sign-free rank-one projectors, and takes the leading eigenvector. The latter two methods ignore the user's choice of written target; the first isolates the fitting criterion and is only an attribution control.
