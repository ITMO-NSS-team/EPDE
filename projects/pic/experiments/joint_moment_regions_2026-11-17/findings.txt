Completed 2026-11-17: solver-free joint weak-moment regions and correlated noise.

The direct residual approach now supplies joint coefficient regions and
explicit boundedness/emptiness certificates. Combining local and broad banks
is a useful partial repair: 57/60 iid reference cases have bounded outer
regions, versus 51/60 local and45/60 broad. Forced KdV remains provably
unbounded for these constraints; KS still accepts the wrong sign of its
fourth-derivative coefficient. Positive noise correlation exposes a separate,
severe failure of the current noise estimator. None of these results supports
replacing dyad by compatibility or uncertainty as the structural objective.

Construction and its limitations
-------------------------------
Use corrected raw weak moments at fixed coefficients, with error matrix E.
S=mean(E_cal^T E_cal) is a second moment, not a centered covariance. On the
symbolically stochastic columns, calibrate c as the empirical95% check
quantile of lambda_max(S^-1/2 E_check^T E_check S^-1/2). Exact deterministic
columns remain in equations and have zero noise. No covariance-rank fallback
was needed in117 records. The common bound is ||E v||^2<=c v^T S v.

Invert v^T(M_obs^T M_obs-cS)v<=0, with the target coefficient fixed to1.
A positive-definite feature block yields an ellipsoid or an empty region;
a negative eigenvalue gives an explicit unbounded feasible direction. This
uses only moment computation and linear algebra. The common-library cutoff
is intentionally simultaneous and can be much wider than directional tests.
Residual noise coverage and acceptance of a zero residual differ when the
clean discrete weak residual is nonzero; both are saved separately.

Combining banks
---------------
Joint cutoff cJ is the95% quantile of max(local_score,broad_score) on shared
check fields. Require both original inequalities with cJ. A nonnegative
mixture of their quadratics is a necessary condition. Choose its weight to
maximize the minimum eigenvalue of the normalized feature block; a scalar
bounded search is checked against endpoints and a101-point grid. This is
a geometric certificate, not a tuned preference for particular coefficients.

An ellipsoid from the mixture is an OUTER bound, not the exact intersection.
Its center need not satisfy both constraints. Before querying true laws,
47/60 references have an explicit common feasible witness. Post-freeze truth
queries and the AC32 fitted witness establish nonemptiness for all60 reference
intersections. All57 bounded outer regions are therefore bounds on nonempty
reference sets; no exact multidimensional intersection shape is claimed.

Across1050 candidates:679 bounded outer certificates,307 certified empty,
62 certified unbounded,2 unresolved geometry.586 have an explicit common
witness;157 have unresolved nonemptiness in the truth-free certificate
procedure. Do not count every nonempty outer ellipse as an accepted model.

Compound is the clearest complementary-information improvement. BOTH banks
alone are unbounded on all3 anchors. Positive mixtures, local weights
0.04646/0.04615/0.04447, give finite outer bounds on all3 intersections.
Seed30 projections are[-1.7568,6.2465] and[0.6067,2.8284]. They remain broad.
For several other systems one already-bounded bank supplies the certificate.

Forced KdV: all3 joint regions remain unbounded. Explicit directions have
strictly negative curvature in BOTH bank constraints, and finite witnesses
are checked directly. This is a property of these moment tests, not proof
that no solver-free method could identify the equation. Directional-cutoff
controls also find unbounded witnesses in each bank separately on all3
anchors, so full-library simultaneous calibration is not the sole cause.
Separate pointwise witnesses do not by themselves prove a joint pointwise ray.

Positive correlation plus assumed iid even makes every local reference

ranking alone cannot repair a misspecified uncertainty model.

Noise estimator diagnosis and tested partial fixes
--------------------------------------------------
Positive-correlation raw-field AR sigma/true_sigma: median0.7657, range
[0.6553,1.0286]. Some axis correlations collapse from true0.4 to aboutzero;
raw selected difference orders range2..58. The theoretical lag-ratio slope
with respect to rho at selected orders ranges0.000263..0.204 (median0.0420),
versus0.5 at order1. High orders amplify lag-ratio sampling error when
inverting for rho. Selecting small amplitudes across orders/axes is another
source of downward selection; the present tests do not isolate its full bias.

Pool amplitude using the estimated correlations and existing selected orders:
median ratio improves0.7657 ->0.9326, but minimum remains0.7123. No coverage
claim is made for this diagnostic; pooling alone is not a complete repair.

Remove clean signal and run the SAME estimator on the actual injected noise:
positive-correlation median sigma ratio is0.6833, not near1. Thus signal
reconstruction cannot be the whole explanation. The variance guard rejects
some accurate low-order noise estimates. For stationary noise on n samples,
E[sample variance with denominator n] = sigma^2 *
  (1 - [n+2*sum_{k=1}^{n-1}(n-k)*rho^k]/n^2).
This is less than marginal variance, especially for positive correlation;
finite sample fluctuations add further differences. Treating estimated
sigma^2>observed within-axis variance as impossible is too strong.

Example AC30, pure noise, axis0: true sigma0.0337684, order1 estimate
sigma0.0335279/rho0.399239, but sigma^2 exceeds observed variance0.00104647;
the guard rejects it. Raw-field high-order selection is also problematic.

Guard-removal ablation on all24 stress anchors: pure positive-noise median
ratio0.6833 ->0.9760, but minimum remains0.6508. For raw fields the selected
sigma is unchanged in all24 records. Therefore simply removing the guard is
NOT a supported fix for the reported coverage failure. No production guard
was removed; its original role in avoiding signal-dominated divergent fits
still needs a statistically valid replacement. Negative-correlation estimates
are much better in these controls. See noise_attribution.json and
noise_guard_diagnostic.json for every case and the raw/pure traces.

Decision and next target
-----------------------
Keep dyad provisionally for structural ranking, with complexity as comparator,
as supported by the prior fresh-seed replacement study. Direct weak residuals
remain a useful experimental path for uncertainty and coefficient precision.
Combined banks are a supported boundedness improvement, not a complete method.

The next focused repair should estimate correlation and amplitude jointly
without selecting the minimum of noisy high-order fits. It must first pass
noise-only checks and then fresh raw-field/coverage tests for iid and both
correlation signs. After calibration, target complementary moments for forced
KdV and KS and report uncertainty widths/sign resolution separately from
structural selection. Do not hide unresolved coefficients behind high coverage,
or turn compatibility into a new reward for redundant equations.
