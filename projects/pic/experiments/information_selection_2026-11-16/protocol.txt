Training-only feature-information selection, 2026-11-16
Initial targeted ac/wave/kdv_cossin/ks, seeds0/1/2, 5% noise.

Candidate fitting bank family, fixed before testing:
  local: previous adaptive continuous weak bank;
  broad0: broad minimum-smoothness bank from broad_moments;
  broad1: its q+1 ablation.
Reuse existing coefficients for both ordinary and corrected least squares.
No candidate is solved, no feature is added, and no coefficients are tuned
against clean or held-out observations.

For each candidate and outer training fold, estimate each bank's operator
noise Gram from the original reconstructed bootstrap ensemble. Bias-correct
the observed training operator mean as before. Let G be its feature Gram
and N=(1+1/B) times the sample feature-error Gram. Select the feasible bank
with the smallest largest generalized eigenvalue of (N,G): minimize the
worst noise-to-observed-energy feature direction. No SNR threshold is chosen.
Rank/zero handling uses the existing numerical cutoff. Empty feature sets
tie and keep the local bank. Break other exact ties in declared bank order.

This criterion is invariant to nonsingular feature rescaling in exact
arithmetic. It does not include target noise, common reconstruction bias,
or coefficient-dependent residual covariance; those are explicit limitations.
Do not interpret a small score as proof of physical identifiability.

The selected bank is candidate-specific for FITTING only. Every candidate
is scored on the SAME unchanged local held-out bank and frozen spectral
control. Preserve dyad and registered equivalences. Compare OLS and CLSM
as separate complete arms; do not select an estimator per system from truth.
Use clean fields only after all selections to diagnose coefficient accuracy.
Compare half/full bootstrap noise-Gram selection as a numerical sensitivity.

Record all choices, generalized ratios, infeasibilities, regression results,
and score-only baselines. A successful targeted test requires broader
core-15 validation before any promotion; a failed test is retained.
