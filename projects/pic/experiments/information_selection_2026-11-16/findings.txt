Feature-information selection: failed criterion, with an error diagnosis
2026-11-16; ac/wave/forced-KdV/KS, three seeds each; all 12 cases completed.

Minimizing the worst predictor-noise direction does not solve weak fitting.
It selects broad banks for KS and inherits their catastrophic coefficient
errors. It helps wave but overlooks target-moment error and its amplification
through regression. No version of this selector should be promoted.

There were no infeasible selections. Half/full covariance budgets change

Post-selection error decomposition:
diagnose_error_budget.py covers all 12 cases and all three banks/folds, using
saved clean fields only as controls. It decomposes the actual corrected-fit
coefficient error into target-moment error, predictor-moment error, clean
discretization residual, and covariance correction. Their coefficient-vector
sum matches the actual error to maximum absolute error 2.17e-12. Their squared
clean-energy impacts are NOT additive because components can cancel.

  trustworthy physical estimate. This differs from the wave/KS target issue.

Implication:
The next fitting criterion must include BOTH target and predictor errors,
their correlations, and amplification into prediction error on a common
weak validation representation. A feature-only SNR criterion, numerical
rank, or coefficient stability can all prefer a precise-looking wrong fit.
Covariance-aware multiscale fitting is a plausible next direction, but its
joint error model and bias control need derivation and validation. It has
not been implemented or claimed as a successful fix in this study.

No production changes were made. These are targeted reused-data experiments,
