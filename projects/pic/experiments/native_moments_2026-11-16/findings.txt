Native-sample quadrature and combined local/broad moments, 2026-11-16

Direct native sampling removes most of the deterministic KS error caused by
dense field resampling. It does not fix broad fitting under noise. Combining
local and broad constraints prevents the large noisy KS regression, but the
local KS fit remains more accurate. Mixed fitting improves the median clean
residual energy on AC, forced KdV and wave relative to the old local baseline;
it is not a universal replacement. Validation-score misranking and inaccurate
forced-KdV coefficients remain unresolved.

Scope and controls

Twelve runs: AC, forced KdV (kdv_cossin), wave and KS, each at 5% independent
Gaussian observation noise with seeds 3/4/5. These are the SAME records as
the preceding EIV experiment, used for paired comparisons, not another
independent replication. Known supports only: 12 reference/seed cases and
24 registered-equation/seed cases including four wave equivalents. Each
case rotates three interleaved groups through training and validation.
This is not a repeat of the 15-system structural screen or a new search.

Frozen protocol.txt specifies native versus interpolated input; local,
broad and combined moment banks; corrected OLS and unchanged 512-draw
residual-covariance IRLS. Combined fitting stacks constraints and retains
their joint covariance from shared draws. No coefficient averaging or
manually tuned bank weight is used. No clean or held-out values select a
bank, fit, geometry, convergence threshold or regularizer. IRLS starts
from corrected OLS, requires full covariance rank, and uses relative
prediction-change tolerance 1e-8 with a 100-iteration limit. Failures are
retained explicitly without a fallback or averaging incomplete cases.

Native quadrature integrates monomials on the original sample grid. It
still uses within-cell polynomial interpolation (degree 3 in time, 5 in
space); it removes dense field resampling, not all interpolation. Remaining
derivative factors use the existing fourth-order central differences, with
support halos. Weak test derivatives are evaluated analytically.

Broad supports were aligned to the native grid, changing physical geometry
for wave and slightly for KS relative to the preceding study. The NEW
interpolated controls have exactly the same physical windows, centers and
frequencies as the native controls. Use those matched controls to attribute
effects to native sampling, rather than comparing directly with old broad
results. Local fitting uses the frozen historical natural-stride subset.

Metric: median over three seeds of the three-fold mean residual energy on
the same frozen clean spectral evaluation bank. Lower is better. This is
neither a relative coefficient error nor a trajectory prediction error.
Clean data are used only after fitting for diagnostic evaluation.

Native mixed IRLS improves 9/12 reference/seed cases versus old local CLSM:
AC 2/3, forced KdV 3/3, wave 3/3, KS 1/3. Median changes are approximately
28%, 50%, and 92% lower energy for the first three systems, but 35% higher
for KS. Combining banks sacrifices some broad-fit accuracy for forced KdV
and wave while preventing the broad-only KS collapse. Interpolated mixed
fitting performs similarly, showing that the main noisy-data benefit here
comes from combining constraints, rather than from native sampling alone.
These small paired samples do not establish population-level superiority.

Clean controls isolate the resampling problem

On matched broad KS geometry, corrected OLS on clean observations gives:
  Full clean sampling:                     1.042770e-10
  Subsampled clean, then dense resampling:  2.069206e-1
  Subsampled clean, native quadrature:      9.943466e-5

Native quadrature reduces this error about 2,081-fold, yet remains far above
the full-sampling floor. Local native clean energy is 2.394381e-8; mixed
native clean energy is 2.394442e-8. This isolates a substantial deterministic
representation effect, but the noisy native broad energy of 1.938218 shows
that eliminating most of that effect is insufficient. The remaining noisy
failure cannot be assigned uniquely to variance, identifiability or noise-
model error by this experiment. The clean controls repeat identical clean
data across seeds and are not independent replicates.

AC's small coefficient also remains poorly resolved: native mixed median
2.434593e-4 versus exact 1e-4. Wave's native mixed median is 0.0368125 versus
exact 0.04; broad native is 0.0394600. A reduced residual alone does not
justify claiming all coefficients have been recovered.

Validation does not yet supply a reliable automatic bank selector

The old common broad validation score was reproduced with maximum error
0.0 for saved baseline fits. New direct-native validation uses common
physical supports inside all groups' usable domains, including partial
native cells at off-grid endpoints. Its 512-draw pooled covariance metric
is learned only from training and is shared across all candidates and fits.
All validation covariance matrices pass the full-rank feasibility check.

Descriptive pairwise ranking agreement with frozen clean energy, across
771 dependent pairs of complete new fits on reference cases:
  Old broad score:          579/771 = 75.1%
  Native broad, whitened:   601/771 = 78.0%
  Native local, whitened:   435/771 = 56.4%
  Native mixed, whitened:   565/771 = 73.3%

Pairs share records and coefficients; these are not 771 independent tests.
Native broad has modestly higher descriptive agreement. Adding local
validation does not improve agreement here. For example, on wave seed 3,
mixed native validation chooses native local IRLS with clean energy
0.298396, whereas the old broad score chooses interpolated broad IRLS with
0.000552725. Thus combined fitting and combined validation must be assessed
separately. All fit-selection summaries in ranking_diagnostic.csv are
post-hoc diagnostics; no selector is promoted, and an outer validation split
would be required to evaluate a selection pipeline.

Failures and numerical checks

426/432 new IRLS fits converge. Six reach the 100-iteration limit:
  Forced KdV seed 3, folds 0 and 1: native and interpolated local (4 fits).
  Wave seed 3, fold 2: native mixed, equivalent_3.
  Wave seed 4, fold 1: interpolated local, equivalent_3.
All reference mixed fits converge; the equivalent-support failure remains
reported. No failure is replaced with a successful subset average. All
432 new corrected-OLS fits succeed. See failed_fits.csv for diagnostics.

Quadrature checks pass: aligned-weight maximum difference 7.28e-13;
off-grid polynomial integration-by-parts error 3.25e-13; agreement between
shifted sampling grids; constant preservation; rejection of unsafe supports.
Three ODE_simple identity controls preserve their additive constants:
  u' cos(t) + u sin(t) = 1
  u cos(t) - u' sin(t) = 1.3
  (u')^2 + u^2 = 2.69
The last identity uses a derivative power; both phase identities do not.
These are clean identity-preservation controls, not a noisy search result.
Completion audit details, source/input/result hashes and replay checks are
recorded in completion_audit.json after running audit_native.py.

Implication for the next experiment

Retain local and broad fits as separate candidates and assess how a
solver-free validation score can distinguish their accuracy on an outer
split. Do not automatically replace the local objective with the mixed
one, or treat the modest ranking-agreement gain as validation of a selector.
For forced KdV, inspect training-side sensitivity and uncertainty of the
coefficient directions: residual reduction has not resolved the coefficient
error. These are proposed follow-ups, not completed experiments here.
