Frozen comparison of solver-free implicit fitting corrections

Primary comparison: current target-symmetric local-plus-time implicit fit with training noise correction off versus on. Each geometry is fixed per system and fold from previous seed-0 designs. Primary windows, block weights, eligible candidate supports, raw groups, held-out matrices, column scales, whitening, validation noise correction and median-pivot dyad stay identical between paired methods. Fit all coefficients anew with a homogeneous real equation. No term-count criterion. A secondary comparison repeats the same off/on switch using the existing broad0 geometry, feasible on every default system and shared across all candidates in that fold. Keep both geometries separate. The original directional pipeline is context for matched observations.

Use the 15 default systems and all 20 registered equation slots. Replay the three previously studied seeds 6111–6113 and separately evaluate new 5% iid field seeds 73001–73006 on each existing clean trajectory. Candidate pools are archived benchmark panels. Treat noise-seed repetitions as conditional on those trajectories.

Use 128 training-group bootstrap noise draws with adaptive reconstruction per draw, shared across methods. Primary blocks are the original local bank and temporal modes 0 through ±4 on the broad validation footprint, each divided by the square root of its row count. The secondary bank is the original historical broad0 on the training group's footprint. Off uses the lowest right singular direction after pilot column normalization. On takes the smallest algebraic eigenvector of the bias- and covariance-corrected Gram with the same pilot column normalization. Preserve negative minima, include deterministic constant columns, abstain on zero scale or a numerically nonunique direction. Audit refits after permutation and signed unit rescaling.

Primary outcomes are survival of any registered law or equivalent on the two-objective Pareto front, the fraction of equation/seed slots admitting at least one unregistered add_* competitor, and the count of surviving such competitors divided by all their panel instances. Add_* identifies a support formed by adding a term to the reference, not a claim about the size of its fitted coefficient. Include literal reference survival, mean front size and paired gains/losses. For unresolved candidates count guaranteed and possible survival explicitly. Track nonregistered survivors separately from the known valid equivalents. Compute reference coefficient angles with the historical energy weighting as a secondary check. Replay previous uncorrected results and the three matched NS deletion comparisons. No tuning using the fresh outcomes, candidate integration or production modification.
