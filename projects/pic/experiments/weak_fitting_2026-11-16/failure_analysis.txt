Failure analysis: fitting, quadrature, and discrepancy are separate issues
2026-11-16; default core-15 saved datasets; no equation solves

1. Short weak windows attenuate the high-derivative coefficients.

The previous window radius was selected from the bandwidth of the observed
field. That controls field reconstruction, not the noise amplification of
third/fourth derivatives and derivative products. On KS, old spatial radii
were 9-10 samples. The training-only PCR selector consistently chose radius
64 with smoothness 5-6. The new fit uses the observed raw training planes,
production integration by parts, and noise/grid-sensitive window selection.

For seed 0, old KS coefficients averaged about [-0.104,-0.0026,-0.021] against
[-1,-1,-1]. The new raw weak CLSM fit reduces clean-field residual energy
from 0.859 to 0.000102. Across all three seeds the median is 0.866 -> 0.0000553.
The primary-noise scale estimate was already close to correct: estimated
sigma 0.05319-0.05344 versus injected sigma 0.05336. Wrong noise amplitude
alone cannot explain this failure. Estimated correlations can still be wrong.

Nonlinear diffusion is especially sensitive to derivative products. After
IBP, u*u_xx contains -u_x^2 plus a second-derivative test of u^2/2. The two
physical terms therefore share a noisy component that should cancel at the
correct coefficients. The old estimates did not recover that cancellation.
New raw weak CLSM lowers the median clean residual energy 0.370 -> 0.00114;
mean correction and covariance correction help here. Yet structural retention
remains 1/3, because its evaluation bank is also noisy (see point 3).

2. Coarse weak quadrature creates a different, noise-free error.

The first adaptive fitting implementation evaluates on the raw modulo-3 time
grid. This is a real change from evaluating on the previous reconstructed
full grid. Even with clean input, the new selected NS momentum windows yield
clean held-out residual energies 0.00927 and 0.00460 (seed 0, means over folds).
The corresponding previous clean full-grid fits gave about 8.1e-6 and 3.4e-6.

Integrating a clean reconstruction over the SAME physical windows on a time
grid three times finer lowers those errors to 0.00132 and 0.000693. On noisy
fields the same control improves 0.0112 -> 0.00275 and 0.00481 -> 0.000821.
This diagnoses a quadrature/discretization contribution, not merely noise or
selection of the wrong physical law. Refinement alone did not recover the old
accuracy; other spatial/derivative and window effects remain.

The production selector can use q larger than the coarse window radius.
Production kernels then explicitly cap exactness at the available number of
moments; they are not exact for all polynomials through the declared 2q.
A follow-up selector requires m_coarse >= q, so 2m_coarse+1 >= 2q+1. This is
a dimension requirement for retaining all those moments, not a tuned cutoff.

This follow-up improved NS seed-0 momentum clean residuals to 0.00247 and
0.000599 under raw CLSM, but still fell short of the old fit. It did not
resolve the AC or forced-KdV failures. It also had NO admissible window for
wave seed 0: a 27x81 training grid, derivative edge crops, full moment order,
and the selector's library row requirement cannot all be met on its existing
window lattice. That failure is reported, not replaced by an oracle-selected
window or hidden fallback. Four seed-0 cases completed; wave was infeasible.
The strict follow-up is not a second full core-15 validation.

3. Accurate coefficients do not make the current held-out discrepancy accurate.

Use the KNOWN coefficients only as a diagnostic, never in fitting. Under the
unchanged evaluation bank, the three forced-KdV cross-product scores are
+1996.56, +1459.51, and -1337.85. The corresponding clean residual energies
are about 3.7e-7. Ordinary energies are 10603, 21045, and 12037. Thus even an
oracle coefficient estimator cannot make this noisy test bank report a small,
stable discrepancy. The sign changes demonstrate severe finite-record
variation; the controls do not separately identify residual bias and variance.

For nonlinear diffusion, the known-law cross-products are -2.236, +4.043,
and -0.497, while clean energy is 8.2e-9. For KS they are -0.0437, +0.0334,
and +0.0133 with clean energy around 4e-11. Negative estimates are retained;
they are not negative physical error or proof of higher precision.

An explicit KS seed-0 ranking reversal remains after fitting is repaired:
the extra-u^2 candidate has a cross-product advantage of 7.54e-6 and lower
frozen dyad, although its clean residual energy is worse by 2.23e-6. This is
evidence about ranking error at this finite record, not a general proof that
every unregistered extra term is false. Registered equivalent laws remain
credited throughout.

4. Small terms and finite-record competitors remain delicate.

AC loses a small diffusion term in some new fits; NS can lose small viscosity
terms. For several such cases, the incomplete model even predicts the clean
field better than the currently biased full-model fit. That is a coefficient
estimation problem before it is a discrepancy problem. Other close rankings
reverse between half/full bootstrap counts. Do not make a structural decision
from a tiny signed discrepancy gap without accounting for measurement and
fit uncertainty. No hand-tuned structural threshold was introduced here.

Implication for the next method revision:
Keep the successful broad weak supports for high derivatives, but require
quadrature convergence separately from noise robustness. Evaluate fitting
windows on a sufficiently resolved reconstruction/integral representation.
Then choose evaluation weak moments by their measured operator uncertainty,
not only the field's DCT bandwidth. A new fit alone cannot fix point 3.
Use the existing frozen evaluation cache as a control when testing that change.
