Solver-free weak fitting experiment, 2026-11-16

Hypothesis: bandwidth chosen for reconstructing the observed field does not
control noise in its weak derivative columns. The previous short-window fits
therefore attenuate physical coefficients. Test a train-only weak-window
selection rule that measures noise amplification and grid sensitivity.

Freeze the previous core-15 candidate panel, raw noise seeds, modulo-3 time
splits, reconstructed evaluation fields, bootstrap biases, spectral bandwidths,
training-target normalization, and the full-record raw dyad axis. Retain both
GCV and LOO evaluation bands. No evolutionary search or equation solves.
Use the previous latest budget: 128 draws for ac, kdv_cossin, pde_compound,
pde_divide and ns; 32 for the other ten. Nested half-count fits assess MC
sensitivity. These are computational budgets, not confidence guarantees.

New fitting operates directly on each raw training grid (complete spatial
planes), avoiding interpolation and smoothing of the regression observations.
Build the same symbolic weak terms using production integration by parts.
Select a common window for every candidate in that training group using the
existing WF.choose_window PCR criterion: perturb the measured data with its
estimated AR noise, compare to step-doubled data, and score coefficient
changes and noise shares. Targets are the actual candidate target columns.
All library columns participate; no known coefficients or clean data enter.

Compare raw weak OLS, mean-corrected weak OLS, and signed CLSM weak regression.
The existing independent reconstruction is used only as a bootstrap pilot.
Bootstrap draws add the estimated measurement noise on the raw training grid
and rebuild weak nonlinear columns without re-smoothing. Their mean and
covariance correct the observed raw weak matrix. Both raw fitting arms and
the old fit are evaluated on IDENTICAL held-out quadratic forms. Verify that
the frozen forms reproduce the previous saved scores before interpreting.

Settings audit:
- Window half-widths, smoothness and stride: production data-driven selector.
- Window candidates: powers-of-two lattice (numerical search structure).
- Smoothness begins at minimum IBP derivative order and extends while the
  best score is on the largest level; production J_GUARD is a compute guard.
- Edge crop: derived from remaining FD stencil reach, doubled for step doubling.
- Rows: production dyad floor at the same maximum candidate width (8).
- Noise sigma, correlations and difference orders: estimated from raw training
  data. Do not use the injected 5% sigma in fitting; it is diagnostic only.
- Gram rank cutoff: existing numerical rcond, no structural penalty or tuning.
- No clipping of negative corrected Gram eigenvalues or residual products.

Primary questions: (1) Are reference coefficients better on the clean field?
(2) Does catalog-valid structural nondominance improve on the unchanged noisy
evaluation? A positive answer to (1) does not imply a positive answer to (2).
Clean fields and known coefficients are diagnostic controls only. Report all
equations separately, credit catalog equivalents, and do not label every
unregistered competitor false.

Matched reconstruction ablation (added after the seed-0 raw-fit probe):
Also fit the independently reconstructed training field on the exact SAME
selected raw-grid window. Reconstruct each bootstrap draw and apply its
mean/covariance corrections there. This separates the adaptive window change
from the removal of smoothing. Raw and reconstructed arms share bootstrap
draws seeded identically to the original study. The final battery includes
both arms for every system; no system-specific method choice is made.

Separate numerical follow-up, after main results:
Require m_coarse >= q in the PCR candidate lattice so quadrature can represent
every declared polynomial moment through 2q on both grids. This constraint is
mathematically derived, not tuned using a ranking. The follow-up is recorded
separately because its feasibility can fail on short training grids. Main
candidate, fit and discrepancy results are not replaced by this diagnostic.
