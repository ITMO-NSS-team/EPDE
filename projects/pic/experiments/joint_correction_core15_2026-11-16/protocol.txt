With centered joint operator draws D and whitening A, corrected normal Gram

a claim of exact unconditional unbiasedness for data-estimated weights.

For a fixed registered support X and training pilot, form the GLS left
inverse L of its validation predictor matrix using training pooled weights.
Apply rich training spectral predictor matrix H: score is
  Re[(H L r_left)^* (H L r_right)] / ||rich pilot target||^2.
Use a real Gram square root for H when computing. Require identifiable
predictor directions in both validation and rich pilot matrices; report
infeasible otherwise. Constants remain actual weak columns.
This score compares fitting methods for the SAME specified equation support.
It projects away residual directions outside that predictor span and is NOT
a general structural-discovery score. This limitation is tested, not hidden.
