Exploratory existing-estimator comparison, declared before its results
====================================================================
The profile-start guard removes extreme basins but moderate positive-rho fits
still overestimate amplitude. Compare the unchanged existing local6 robust
quintic-contrast estimator against repaired temporal estimates on ALL96 survey
fields. This tests a different signal/noise separation assumption: locally
quintic signal removal, rather than stochastic curvature AR2 modeling.
No selection/tuning/oracle starts; fit every field with the historical estimator
and retain boundary/nonconverged results. This is exploratory attribution, not
new calibration or proof that local6 should replace the temporal estimator.
