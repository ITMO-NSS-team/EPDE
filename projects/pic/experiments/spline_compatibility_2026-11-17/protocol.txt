Keep the previously frozen candidate cubic splines p=8,16,24,32,48,64 and
conditional four-fold Student-t selection score. New compatible-CV rule:
evaluate every candidate's (sigma,rho) on the SAME error-contrast space,
orthogonal to the p64 mean basis. Compare its restricted Gaussian negative

the unchanged CV and the fixed-p64 estimator. Reuse all fits; no truth in choices.

avoiding a transplanted basic-bootstrap bias correction. Region for (sigma,rho):
restricted likelihood-ratio <= cutoff. Report nominal chi-square2(.975) and a
finite-grid model-calibrated cutoff. No ellipse approximation or point scan is
treated as the full region. Region membership is evaluated at truth only after
the fitted models and calibration cutoff have been frozen.

Report paired noise bias/absolute error, changes in selected p, selected-fit
boundary/unresolved counts, rich-model likelihood coverage, and noncompact
noise regions. For rho->1 and sigma^2*(1-rho)->eta>0, error-contrast covariance
tends to eta*K, K=-Q_perp' |i-j| Q_perp, since the spline mean contains a constant.

certifies an escaping sequence with sigma->infinity, hence no finite upper
noise-amplitude bound. Near equality is unresolved, not declared bounded.
Failure of this sufficient test alone is not a certified compactness proof.
Validate the limit with dense contrasts and finite-rho sequences; report
limiting likelihood below the stored optimum as a separate optimization warning.
