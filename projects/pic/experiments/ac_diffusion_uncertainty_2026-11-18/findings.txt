Keep both the full and reaction-only supports eligible on this trajectory at 5% noise. The reaction-only fit usually has the lower held-out discrepancy, but the preference changes across repeat measurements. All six observation-specific forecasts span zero. Fresh benchmark repeats

The sampling unit is a whole field. A positive margin D(reaction only) − D(full) favors diffusion. Each of 128 independent noise realizations used the original trajectory and the complete corrected local-plus-time weak fitting procedure. Every rotation re-estimated the group noise model, reconstruction, coefficient corrections, whitening, scale and coefficients. Both candidates shared the same observations and random perturbations.

Forecast check

Each of the six original observations supplied its own reconstructed pilot and fitted noise model. A parametric bootstrap with 128 independent full-field refits per observation produced an approximate central 95% repeat range (order statistics 3 and 126). All six original point scores favored the reduced law; every range included both signs. Five ranges contained all 128 independent benchmark margins. The sixth contained 126. The ranges were 1.42 to 4.52 times as wide as the empirical benchmark repeat range. These checks reuse the same 128 fresh fields for each forecast, so coverage counts across forecasts cannot be combined as independent trials.

Generator diagnostic

The fitted spatial AR correlation varied from 0.088 to 0.576 across the six observations; true benchmark noise is iid. For seed 73001, a matched four-cell diagnostic reused 64 identical innovation streams per cell. Around its reconstructed pilot, switching from fitted AR noise to the known benchmark iid noise reduced the interquartile width of the margin from 3.40e-5 to 1.14e-5. Around the clean signal it reduced the width from 2.40e-5 to 6.48e-6. With iid noise, replacing the pilot by the clean signal also changed the number of full-law wins from 39/64 to 22/64. Both noise and pilot choice matter here. These controls used the benchmark signal or noise specification and do not establish a calibrated data-only replacement.

Implication for search

For this pair, report the diffusion deletion as unresolved at the repeat-measurement threshold. Eliminating the full law from the earlier Pareto front would hide a term that the field supports on many repeats. The next discriminating check for an uncertainty-aware comparison is the analogous Navier–Stokes deletion, where the previous point scores favored retaining viscosity. A blanket all-system pruning rule would require calibration beyond this trajectory and support pair.
