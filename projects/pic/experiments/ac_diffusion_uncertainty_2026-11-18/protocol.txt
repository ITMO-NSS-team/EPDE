Paired repeat-measurement uncertainty for Allen–Cahn diffusion

Compare the registered full law with e0:drop_0, which drops diffusion and refits both reaction coefficients. The primary statistic is D(reaction only) minus D(full) from the noise-corrected local-plus-time implicit fit, averaged over the three fixed training rotations. A positive difference favors diffusion. Both candidates use the same realization, training perturbations, held-out groups, and whitening in each rotation. Keep the previous window designs and candidate sets. The sampling unit is the entire noisy field. The rotations share data and are never treated as independent replicates.

Use each of the six previously studied fields (73001–73006) as a separate anchor. Reconstruct its full-field pilot by the existing GCV method and fit the existing separable AR noise model to that observation. For each anchor generate 128 independent noisy fields around that pilot. Within every outer field re-estimate the group noise models, adaptive reconstruction, coefficient-bias and covariance corrections (128 training draws per fold), pooled whitening (128 additional draws per fold), pilot normalization, both sets of coefficients, and common held-out discrepancy. No reused anchor corrections or frozen fitted coefficients. Freeze only geometry, support, and method. Monte Carlo seed and raw noise field are shared between full and reduced fits. Reproduce the six previous observations before interpreting forecasts.

From each anchor's 128 paired differences take order statistics 3 and 126 as an approximate central 95% repeat-measurement range. The endpoint is the score of a new realization of this analysis on the same underlying signal, not a confidence interval for a physical coefficient or mean. If the range straddles zero the score does not give a repeatable deletion sign at that level. Separately, evaluate the complete procedure on 128 brand-new benchmark fields with the same stored clean trajectory and 5% iid noise, seeds 750001–750128. Report empirical signs, uncertainty in the fresh sign fraction, and the fraction of these independent fields covered by each previously fixed anchor range. All six ranges are checked against the same 128 observations. Do not pool the anchors' coverage trials as independent. No tuning from the new seeds.

For interpretation also compare the two laws at their registered reaction coefficients, the fitted reduced law against the registered full law, and the full fitted diffusion coefficient. Fixed-coefficient comparisons are benchmark-only diagnostics. Save per-fold scores and coefficients; reconstruct all margins and interval endpoints independently. This is a one-trajectory, one-deletion study. Neither dyad nor a term-count objective enters the discrepancy contrast. No candidate equation is integrated.
