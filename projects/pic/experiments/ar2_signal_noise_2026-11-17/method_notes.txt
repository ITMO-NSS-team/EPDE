AR2 signal-curvature / AR1 noise: implementation notes
====================================================

Scope
-----
Research-only estimator of observation-noise covariance from a single field.
No candidate differential equation is integrated. The fit operates on the
observed field, before candidates, clean truth or evaluation draws are used.
The model of curvature is a nuisance representation, not a claim that physical
signal derivatives are stationary random processes. The same representation
and starts are used on every system. Constants and linear trends are annihilated
by second differences; constants and valid identities remain in the candidates.

Model and exact likelihood
--------------------------
For each axis, d = D2 y and
Cov(d) = sigma^2 [D2 R_AR1(rho) D2' + kappa R_AR2(p1,p2)].
R matrices have unit diagonal. The AR2 partial correlations p1,p2 imply
a1=p1*(1-p2), a2=p2, innovation variance (1-p1^2)*(1-p2^2).
The correlation sequence is gamma0=1, gamma1=p1,
gamma_j=a1*gamma_(j-1)+p2*gamma_(j-2).
One sigma is shared across axes of a variable. rho,kappa,p1,p2 vary by axis.
Axis likelihoods are added; for multidimensional data this is a composite
likelihood. Its Hessian is not an independent-axis parameter covariance.

Filtering by A=I-rho L and B=I-a1 L-a2 L^2 preserves determinant 1.
Cov(B A d)/sigma^2 has bandwidth four, including exact finite boundaries.
Its stationary noise polynomial is
[1, -2-a1, 1+2*a1-a2, -a1+2*a2, -a2].
For lag l, multiply the overlapping polynomial inner product by 1-rho^2.
The first three columns require boundary corrections. They are constructed
from the exact bandwidth-two covariance of A D2 noise, with h=1-rho^2:
diagonal0=2*(1-rho)*(3-rho), remaining diagonals=6h;
first off-diagonal=-h*(4-rho), remaining first off-diagonals=-4h;
second off-diagonals=h. Apply B on both sides, including initial boundaries.

The AR2 signal innovations have initial block
T00=1, T01=p1*p2, T11=1-p1^2*(1-p2^2),
and subsequent innovation variance v=(1-p1^2)*(1-p2^2).
The signal contribution is kappa A T A', computed with these initial entries.
This yields an exact banded Cholesky evaluation, not a large-sample Toeplitz
or periodic approximation. fit.py and score_fit.py contain the same model.

Given total quadratic q, contrast count N and log determinant l, the profiled
sigma^2=q/N and normalized objective is log(q/N)+l/N. Data contrasts are scaled
by their RMS for numerical unit stability, then sigma is restored to data units.

Optimization and numerical repair
--------------------------------
Bounds: rho,p1,p2 in [-1+1e-6,1-1e-6], log(kappa) in [-40,20].
Four starts: the observed-field AR1-curvature fit with p2=0, plus fixed
(rho,log(kappa),p1,p2)=(0,-5,.99,-.8),(.4,-3,.95,-.5),(-.4,-11,.9,0).
The best finite successful optimizer result is selected. Noise rho within
1e-5 of +/-1 or log(kappa)>19.99 makes the result explicitly incomplete.
Signal-correlation boundaries are reported, but do not alone invalidate noise.
All starts, termination messages, objectives, boundary flags and source hashes
are retained. There is no guarantee of global or strict local optimality.

The initial implementation used central finite differences, step1e-4,
maxiter300, ftol1e-12, gtol2e-6, maxls40. It produced a VDP noise-boundary
failure on the screen despite lower-objective unsuccessful starts. A separate
version uses an analytic likelihood score, maxiter500 and the same other
tolerances/starts/bounds. Both versions and their results are retained.

For filtered covariance C and field z, s=C^-1 z,
dq = 2 dz' s - s' dC s, dl = trace(C^-1 dC).
The profile gradient is dq/q + dl/N, summed over axis replicates.
Only the inverse entries within the covariance band are needed. They are
computed by backward selected-inverse recursion from the banded Cholesky.
Polynomial covariance derivatives use complex-step1e-30, before Cholesky;
field-filter derivatives are analytic. No complex-step Cholesky is used.
Dense covariance, nested AR1 reduction, selected inverse and score checks
are archived. The synthetic unit/trend check uses the original tolerance.

Optimizer success is an output status, not a stationary-point certificate.
The retrospective projected-gradient diagnostic is theta-clip(theta-g,bounds)
in the optimizer's stated coordinates. Its scale depends on parameterization.
Fourteen of84 primary variable fits exceed1e-3 in max absolute mapping.
Six selected fits have objective more than1e-5 above an unsuccessful start.
These remain in the frozen primary test; outcomes are not replaced post hoc.

Coverage experiment and interpretation
-------------------------------------
Three fresh fitted anchors per system/profile (39,40,41), 5% added Gaussian
noise. IID all15 systems; AR1 correlations +/-0.4 on AC, forced KdV, KS, wave.
For each complete model:256 calibration,256 check,256 actual evaluation fields.
Known-family controls estimate amplitude, fixing rho to0 or its generating
colored-noise value. Noise parameters remain fixed during coverage calibration;
parameter-estimation uncertainty is not propagated in this round.
The native weak library, pilot reconstruction, local/broad banks, correction
algebra and matrix confidence regions are inherited unchanged. Fits, metrics,
cutoffs and regions are frozen before clean evaluation. Hashes verify pairing.
The 256 evaluation draws share a fitted anchor, and therefore do not supply
256 independent estimates of unconditional coverage.

Oracle-correction attribution changes only actual-field Gaussian correction
to the true noise covariance, retaining the fitted metric/cutoff/calibration.
It diagnoses mean-correction error; it is not an implementable estimator.
Axis-specific fits on old anchor36 likewise diagnose signal/noise allocation.
Clean curvature concentration and true-sigma ratios are evaluation diagnostics,
never criteria for selecting an axis in the tested estimator.

The analysis harness initially expected a nested dictionary named ar2; the
estimator uses that key for the AR2 coefficient list. analyze_final.py repairs
only this reporting mismatch. The original harness is preserved and the
transformation is hashed in final_analysis_provenance.json. No fit, region,
cutoff, primary result or independent audit changes as a result.
