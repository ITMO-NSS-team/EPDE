Broad raw validation with a common noise-covariance metric
Completed 2026-11-16. Entirely solver-free; experimental fixed-panel study.

The best tested combination retains a registered law or equivalent in 56/60
equation/seed cases, versus the previous 54/60. It combines the previously
saved joint-risk coefficient fits with q0 broad raw validation and a common
training-only covariance metric. Gains are KS seed 0 and forced KdV seed 2;
there are no losses relative to the previous local-score baseline.

This is progress in structural retention, not proof of accurate equation
recovery. KS's new margin is small relative to conditional noise. Forced
KdV's gain has a larger margin but its fitted coefficients remain poor.
All comparisons reuse three noisy records per system, at 5% noise. No new
evolutionary search or independent replication has been performed.

What changed and what was held fixed

The prior raw-local validation experiment had excessive variance. This study
evaluates the same coefficients on broad weak moments over the common
interior of the three raw sample domains. AC, previously infeasible on some
fixed local supports, is now feasible without extrapolation. Interpolation
and Gaussian corrections are fixed; validation does not smooth raw data.

For each training rotation, the weak geometry is chosen by the previous
training-only numerical-rank rule. q0 is minimum weak-test smoothness, q1
adds one order. Fourier modes increase only as required by the fixed panel.
The real moment basis preserves the full complex-conjugate inner product.

For each bank, the training pilot and estimated raw-noise model produce 128
operator perturbations. Their centered, column-normalized row covariances
are pooled into one C shared by all candidate equations. The validation
score is <W r_left, W r_right> / ||W pilot_target||^2, with W=C^(-1/2).
The two residuals come from held-out raw groups. Target and predictor noise
both enter C. No candidate-dependent residual covariance or tuned ridge is
used. The 64-draw prefix is a sensitivity control. All covariances passed the
predefined full-rank numerical check; no null direction was discarded.

The dyad, candidate panels, registered equivalents and coefficients remain
unchanged. Baseline fits are the old local continuous CLSM fits. Joint fits
are from joint_prediction_risk_2026-11-16, where training-only conditional
joint target/predictor prediction risk selected among six existing fits.
No new coefficient estimator is introduced by this validation experiment.

All 15 systems completed at seeds 0, 1 and 2: 45 system runs, 20 equation
slots per seed, 60 equation/seed cases. Targeted wave/KS/AC/forced-KdV runs
are a subset of those 45, not additional independent observations.

KS seed 0 is a tentative gain: its closest gap is only 0.81 SD, and the
reference-plus-u alternative is also within one SD. The clean full-bank
gap against adding u^3 is +3.369e-6, consistent in direction but small.
The 64-draw uncertainty estimate is 4.885e-6 for this closest comparison.

Forced KdV seed 2 has gap/SD about 7.20 against dropping u*u_x. The clean
full-bank gap is +0.057174, agreeing with the observed +0.058893. However,
the frozen clean residual energy of the retained reference fit is 0.076932.
For terms [u*u_x, u_xxx, cos(t)sin(x)], the true coefficients are [-6,-1,1].
The three frozen training-fold fits are approximately:
  [10.31154, -0.30340,  0.18364]
  [ 2.49377, -0.10640, -0.06412]
  [-1.47782, -0.33486,  0.20059]
Survival of that support must not be reported as successful recovery of
these coefficients. The conditional SD also omits their fitting uncertainty.

The four remaining failures are AC seeds 0/2 and forced KdV seeds 0/1.
For AC seed 0, removing the tiny diffusion term wins even on the clean
field with the frozen fitted coefficients (clean gap -0.00014461). This
cannot be repaired solely by reducing stochastic validation variance.
For AC seed 2, the extra harmonic wins noisily despite losing on the clean
field (+0.00011056). Dropping diffusion also narrowly wins, with observed
gap -6.614e-7 and clean gap -8.984e-7. More than one failure mechanism exists.
For forced KdV seeds 0/1, the leading incorrect alternatives also beat the
frozen reference fit on clean data: gaps -0.35072 and -0.23699. Coefficient
bias and the validation representation still need attention.

Saved-fit ranking diagnostics

Separately, score the six already fitted reference options on this new
validation bank. This assesses discrimination but is NOT a new independently
validated selection pipeline. Using the lowest score to choose a fit would
consume the validation groups and require an outer evaluation split.

q0 white128 ranks a more accurate option ahead of local CLSM on all three
wave records, while preserving local CLSM on all KS and AC records:
  Wave seed 0: broad0_OLS,  frozen energy 0.077635 -> 0.00019954
  Wave seed 1: broad0_CLSM, frozen energy 0.156958 -> 0.01242290
  Wave seed 2: local_OLS,   frozen energy 0.011719 -> 0.00196640
The corresponding score gaps / paired SD are -0.12062 / 0.07756,
-0.30478 / 0.12938, and -0.03522 / 0.01012. These margins are diagnostics,
not post-selection confidence intervals. Across these three records,
median frozen wave energy is 0.001966 versus baseline 0.077635.

Across all 60 cases the diagnostic lowest-score option improves frozen
accuracy in 21, worsens it in 7, and leaves it unchanged in 32 relative to
local CLSM. Regressions include all three unforced KdV records, Lorenz
seed 1 slots 0/2 and seed 2 slot 2, and inviscid Burgers seed 2. This is
another reason not to promote this score as a universal fitting selector.
Forced KdV picks local CLSM on all three records, leaving its substantial
coefficient error. Actual joint-fit coefficients used for the 56/60 count
have not changed; do not combine that count with the diagnostic wave gains
as though they came from one new fitted pipeline.

Noise and clean controls

Broad raw moments substantially reduce the previous raw-local variance in
some difficult cases. For wave seed 0, the exact-coefficient conditional
SD falls from 4.140 in the previous raw-local study to 0.13885 with broad
unweighted moments. Its whitened SD is 0.95350. Whitening changes the
metric and need not minimize absolute SD; useful pairwise discrimination
is the relevant check. SD ratios between metrics are not accuracy ratios.

On KS, full clean exact-coefficient energies in the whitened broad metric
are about 6e-14, while interpolation of clean subsamples gives about 2e-7.
For forced KdV, clean cross products can be slightly negative because the
two deterministic interpolation errors differ. Signed cross products are
preserved, not clipped. Clean results were computed after geometry, weights
and coefficients were frozen; none were used to select those quantities.

Constants and the user's ODE_simple dataset

The real-basis transform and common weighting preserve each constant's
actual modulated weak column; no all-ones substitution, centering or global
constant ban is introduced. The separate ODE_simple control uses
u=sin(t)+1.3*cos(t), not the core registry's different forced-oscillator ode.
Its valid identities remain:
  u' cos(t) + u sin(t) = 1
  u cos(t) - u' sin(t) = 1.3
  (u')^2 + u^2 = 2.69
All use derivative order at most 1. Only the energy law needs derivative
power 2. In q0/q1, plain/white128 controls, the largest clean residual energy
is 1.123e-12. Deleting the necessary constants with other coefficients fixed
raises that energy to 3.51-4.30. These are identity-preservation controls,
not extra independent search experiments. No accepted identity is removed.

Checks, artifacts and interpretation

Real-basis isometry, feature-unit invariance, full-rank covariance handling,
constant-column preservation and ODE_simple controls pass. All 45 cases and
all validation arms are feasible. Maximum empirical whitening identity
error is 2.810e-11. Source/input hashes and every saved perturbation array
are audited; coefficients and dyad are verified against frozen inputs.
See completion_audit.json for the final machine-readable checks.

protocol.txt records the method before outcomes. run_broad_validation.py and
moments.py implement it; summarize.py and analyze_margins.py produce the
tables and figure. core_primary_margins.csv contains paired comparisons;
core_closest_eligible_competitor.csv summarizes reference competitors;
margin_summary.json records all arm-specific gains/losses and example fits.
validation_comparison.png/.svg displays structural counts and the separate
saved-fit ranking diagnostic. All 45 raw JSON/NPZ pairs remain in results/.

Covariance weighting has a methodological precedent in weak-form inference
(WENDy, https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.13271; WSINDy,
https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10327340). This experiment implements a
common operator-noise validation metric, not WENDy's candidate-specific
iteratively reweighted parameter fitting. Those papers do not validate
the particular metric or outcomes reported here.

Next step: freeze q0 white128 with the saved joint-risk fitting rule and
evaluate fresh noise seeds before considering production use. Any fit
selection based on the new validation score needs a separate outer split.
Keep structural retention and coefficient accuracy as separate outcomes.
The remaining AC/forced-KdV cases need coefficient-bias/representation

no candidate differential equation was solved, and no commit was created.
