the two-bank feasible sets across scenarios, plus both primary feasible sets.
An intersection over nuisance scenarios would incorrectly require every noise
model to hold. Retain all points, constants and primary regions. Certify every
component independently. A union is bounded if every component has a bounded
outer enclosure; it is unbounded if any component has a certified feasible ray.
Coordinate extrema of bounded component enclosures give a union outer box,
not exact coordinate intervals of the joint feasible set. This diagnostic
tests conservatism of scalar compression only on the fixed reference support.
It is not a new validated discovery algorithm or a calibrated confidence set.
Primary coverage/cutoffs and all data are kept unchanged. The nuisance point
set is finite and may miss truth, even if its convex hull contains truth.
