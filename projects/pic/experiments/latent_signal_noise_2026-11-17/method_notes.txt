Latent-signal/noise research, 2026-11-17

For an axis curve, let D be the second-difference matrix. Model
  D y = D epsilon + curvature,
  Cov(epsilon) = sigma^2 R(rho),
  Cov(curvature) = sigma^2 kappa R(phi).
R(r) is the finite stationary correlation matrix r^abs(i-j). The original
white-curvature model replaces R(phi) with I. Constant and linear signal
trends disappear under D. Neither a candidate differential equation nor its
solution is used. Constants in the weak candidate library remain intact.

The AR filters A(rho)=I-rho L and A(phi)=I-phi L have determinant one.
Their product converts the contrast covariance to bandwidth three. Exact
finite-boundary corrections are included; stationary interior formulas alone
would be incorrect. Banded Cholesky gives q=z' B^-1 z and logdet(B).
With multiple axes/curves, q and logdet are summed and sigma^2=q/count is
profiled. The normalized objective is log(q/count)+logdet/count. This is an
axis composite likelihood on a multidimensional field. Axis/curve dependence
means its naive Hessian is NOT a justified covariance-parameter confidence
matrix. Calibration uses full separable AR fields with the fitted parameters.

The signal prior is a working model. A deterministic oscillating PDE field
need not have stationary AR curvature, and amplitude/correlation estimates can
remain biased. Pure-noise recovery tests identify lost information or numerical
problems; they do not validate performance in the presence of an unknown signal.
The exact zero-signal AR fit is an oracle diagnostic only, never a raw-field
production estimator. Three starts and reported convergence do not establish
a global optimum or statistically identified signal/noise decomposition.

Numerical development record
----------------------------
Initial correlated_fit_initial.py used forward finite differences for the
optimizer. A unit/trend check found about 0.31% amplitude disagreement caused
by sensitivity of the ill-conditioned band likelihood to small differences.
The frozen correlated_fit.py uses three-point central differences with relative
step 1e-4. The original invariance tolerance was retained (8e-4), not loosened.
Both implementations are retained. All correlated screening and fresh coverage
use the corrected version; original RW2 results use latent_fit.py unchanged.
Dense covariance/quadratic/logdet checks cover both versions of the model.

Fresh coverage design and interpretation
---------------------------------------
All noise estimates, reconstructed pilot fields, 256 calibration fields,
256 check fields, matrix metrics and confidence-region certificates are frozen
before inspecting the noiseless field or the 256 independent evaluation fields.
The pilot, native local/broad banks, moment corrections, candidate libraries and
region geometry code are inherited without changes to isolate the noise model.
Nominal 95% operator coverage is simultaneous over coefficient directions within
the fixed term library. It is not a probability of structural correctness.
An accepted true coefficient vector does not establish narrow identification;
unbounded sets and same-target zero-extended supersets must be reported.
Coverage with fixed fitted noise parameters is conditional on the anchor fit;
three anchors do not establish general unconditional 95% validity. The many
evaluation fields are not additional independent fitted anchors.
