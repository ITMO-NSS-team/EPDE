All15 default systems, fresh anchor seeds24/25/26, 5% independent benchmark
noise. Known registered supports/equivalents only; this round tests moment
calibration, not evolutionary structure recovery. Constants are retained.
No candidate ODE/PDE integration, PINNs, ridge, tuned penalty or clean-data
choice of geometry/estimator. Seven worker processes, one BLAS thread each.

Use full native measurement grids. Two fixed banks: local physical windows
from historical seed0 base_plan, and one common-interior broad window with
frequencies 0,+/-1,+/-2 per axis. Use native_quadrature's degree3 time and
degree5 spatial interpolation for integration, analytic test derivatives,
and existing finite differences for factors not moved by integration by
parts. Keep the historical full-panel q/cut geometry, and compute only
columns needed by registered laws plus an explicit constant. This is not
a comparison with differently sampled prior training folds.

For each bank compute ordinary raw weak moments and Gaussian-contraction
corrected moments, using the full-record estimated separable AR noise model.
Degree<=3 polynomial contractions are exact in expectation if the noise
model is correct. They do not invert a noisy coefficient-estimation map.
Reconstruct a GCV pilot only to simulate error variance and joint shape.

256 pilot-plus-noise draws estimate residual variance at any fixed vector
v (target coefficient1). Another256 independent pilot draws calibrate the
95th percentile of mean_r[(weak-error_r @ v)^2 / variance_r(v)]. Errors
are measured relative to the uncorrupted pilot moments. Do not subtract an
empirical coefficient bias or invert the weak Gram matrix for this test.
Marginal standardization is only the statistic; empirical shared-field
draws preserve joint dependence. No chi-square or row-independence claim.
Exact zero variance plus nonzero residual is infinite, zero/zero is zero;
record nonfinite values explicitly, never clip or silently omit.

Compare to256 independent actual benchmark measurements, seeds
1200000+1000*anchor+b. Freeze bank/noise/pilot/calibration arrays before
evaluating clean moments or exact coefficient controls. Calibration RNG
SeedSequence[830140,anchor], check RNG[830141,anchor]. Every bank/method/
candidate receives the same field in each draw.

Primary diagnostic: coverage of the clean discrete residual vector by the
noise region at each registered exact coefficient vector. Separate from
acceptance of a zero residual: the latter includes deterministic data and
quadrature error and may fail even with good noise coverage. Report clean
residual/noise scale and standardized mean-noise shift explicitly.

Also freeze unweighted raw and corrected least-squares fits on the anchor
for each bank/support, using existing unit-scaled rank checks without a
fallback. Evaluate the same conditional noise coverage and zero-residual
acceptance for those fixed vectors on independent measurements. Anchor-fit
acceptance is data-dependent and is not advertised as honest post-selection
coverage. Report deficient fits rather than averaging successful subsets.

Primary is corrected moments, uncorrected moments a matched control.
Neither score is a structural objective or pruning gate. Exact constants
and ODE_simple phase/energy identities receive synthetic controls. Verify

Audit source/input/array hashes and independent saved-array arithmetic.

Only after these checks may confidence-set inversion be explored. A small
noise-normalized residual alone cannot identify coefficients; sets can be
broad, disconnected or unbounded. Any follow-up must be recorded separately.
