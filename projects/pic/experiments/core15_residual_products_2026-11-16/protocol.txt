Core-15 residual cross-product screening protocol (2026-11-16)

Scope: all datasets.names('core'), unmodified default saved arrays. No solver,
trajectory integration, new evolutionary search, or production-code change.
5% additive Gaussian measurement noise; seeds 0,1,2. Each equation is reported
separately. Registered alternative laws are credited. The finite candidate
panel is a diagnostic, not a claim of exhaustive structural recovery.

Split raw time frames modulo 3, retaining complete spatial planes. Every group
is reconstructed independently; each group in turn trains coefficients and
selects the test bandwidth, while the other two evaluate complete residuals.
Keep the common interior reached by every group and by required FD stencils.

PDE adaptation: natural cubic smoothing splines in time, with a pooled GCV
parameter per field, and separable spatial DCT fourth-order shrinkage selected
by pooled GCV. Bootstrap reselects every smoothing parameter and rebuilds
nonlinear products. This differs from the original scalar Kepler smoother.
Noise is estimated from each raw group's differences by the existing AR model.
Known clean fields and coefficients are used only in diagnostic controls.

Fixed common mathematical test smoothness: q=3 in time and q=5 in space,
supporting time derivatives through order 2 and space derivatives through 4.
Local fitting support is the selected LOO spectral half-wavelength, bounded
below by moment exactness and above by the available interval. Row strides
are reduced until the production dyad row floor is met. The full-record raw
dyad uses the same support rule and production dyad_scores, frozen across
discrepancy arms. This is not a replication of the old Kepler CLSM dyad.

Local training coefficients use signed CLSM corrected moments. The spectral
evaluation bank is a full tensor product of modulated weak bumps over the
common interval, including mixed signs in multiple dimensions. Training-only
DCT projection GCV and exact LOO bandwidths are reported separately. Residual
mean bias is estimated by independent group bootstrap, including smoothing.
Cross-product: Re <r_A,r_B> / ||training target||^2, with no clipping.
Matched control: (||r_A||^2+||r_B||^2)/(2 ||training target||^2).

32 bootstrap draws is an initial computational budget, not a statistical
threshold. Compare the nested 16-draw and 32-draw estimates; rerun selected
unstable cases at higher counts. Three noise seeds and three reused rotations
are not nine independent records. This screening does not provide a full
uncertainty interval over training, bandwidth and bias estimation.

Candidate menu, applied per equation: reference, catalog alternatives,
drop-one-term variants, add-one variants (constant, fields, squares, cubes,
time, time squared, sin(2t), cos(2t)), time-polynomial and harmonic fits,
field-plus-harmonic fits, and a first-time-derivative polynomial/harmonic fit.
Exact duplicate term structures are removed. A non-catalog candidate is called
unregistered, not automatically false. Coefficients are refitted for all.
